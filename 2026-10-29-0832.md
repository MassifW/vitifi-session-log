# Spray recommendations, first version — 29 October 2026

Mike: "the spray diary recommendations aren't working yet. We have been recording growth stages", then "we do now have api data for the bannockburn vineyard", and "Yes, build on it" (the Bannockburn weather branch).

**Decided (Mike):** the first version has all four parts: what is due now, cover running out, best spray days, disease risk.

**Found:** the Bannockburn own-logger row on live has its Harvest id and the key exists, but live holds 0 readings from it. The address on main (`getlatestdata`) returns Harvest's homepage; the working reader (api.php, sensor by sensor, per-station trace_map) is on `claude/bannockburn-weather-data-cc28d0`, unmerged and unreleased. The mapped sensors: temperature, humidity, rain, wind, gusts. No leaf wetness. Products carry no re-spray interval or wash-off figure.

**Built** (branch `claude/intelligent-cannon-796463`, which merges the weather branch):
- `app/lib/sprayAdvice.ts` (plain module): `dueNow` (a round is due when the block's latest EL reading has reached its stage, or its resolved date has come; "soon" within a week; contingent and already-sprayed items left out; a block past the window is said to be), `sprayDays` (runs of two hours or more within limits, dry for the hours after; why a day has none), `gublerThomas`, `downyPrimary` (10:10:24, shoots taken as 10 cm from EL 12), `botrytisWetness` (wet = rain or RH >= 90, at 15-25 C; low before EL 19).
- `app/lib/sprayAdviceActions.ts`: the Season Plan, this season's growth readings, each vineyard's model hours (7 days back, 5 ahead) with the station's own hours standing in where it has them.
- `RecommendationsTab.tsx` replaces the placeholder. Manual, chronology, file directory, open items updated. `tests/spray-advice.test.mjs` (12); the growth-stages test that read the old placeholder's words now reads the new tab.

**Checked:** typecheck, build; `npm test` without the live suites 2027 pass, 1 fail (the placeholder test, then fixed). Run against live's data by a read-only script: Pisa EL 15, downy high (10:10:24 met 25 Oct, 12 mm at 15.8 C); Clos Miki EL 9, all low; spray windows on 31 Oct and 3 Nov. **Not seen in a browser**: the local app asks for a sign-in.

**Assumed, not confirmed:** the spray limits are starting figures, shown as such; Harvest's rain readings are taken as per-interval and summed per hour. Botrytis is always marked an estimate.
