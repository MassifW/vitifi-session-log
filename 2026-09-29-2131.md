# 2026-09-29 21:31 — Growth stages, recorded on the walk

*Block layouts, vine counts, yields, input products and rates, client
names and certification details are withheld from this public copy.
Here that means parcel names and the season plan's products.*

Mike asked for growth-stage entry next. Budburst was under way, and every
phenology reading in the app had been imported from the monitoring
sheets — nothing could record one, though the season plan's spray dates
and any real recommendation start from them. Applied to the live
database with his yes; not yet released.

## Found first

- **The groundwork was already there.** A reading is an observation event
  with its stage, a share and an EL number, and the season plan's
  estimation already reads exactly that shape. So new readings feed the
  plan without changing the estimates.
- **One unknown stage name would stop the season plan loading** (the stage
  registry throws, deliberately). The database now refuses anything but
  the five stages it knows.
- **The estimates read every reading, replaced or not** — harmless while
  nothing had ever corrected one, wrong the moment anything did. They also
  read without paging, against a 1,000-row cap.
- **The plan's parcels and the monitored parcels don't fully line up**:
  whole-vineyard plan items borrow from the blocks inside them, and one
  block lies almost wholly outside its vineyard's outline, so its readings
  don't reach those items. Raised with Mike, not changed.

## Built

- **A Growth Stages tab.** "Record a walk": the day, the vineyard, the
  stage, then a share typed against each parcel walked — the monitoring
  sheet's own shape. Parcels watched before come first, each with its last
  reading; a share lower than last time gets a gentle note, never a
  refusal. The EL number fills itself in only where the vineyard's own
  records put one (plus the E-L system's own flowering points) and can be
  changed.
- **Saved all or nothing, as whoever is signed in**, and sent twice lands
  once; with no signal the walk is held on the phone and sends itself when
  signal returns. One reading of a stage per parcel per day — a second is a
  correction and has to say so.
- **Correct and remove**, as new records that say what was wrong; the old
  version stays readable, and the season plan now reads only readings as
  they stand, in pages.
- A database self-test of 19 checks (throwaway logins, rolled back), the
  manual's section, and the Spray Diary's recommendations note now points
  at the new tab.

## Verified

On a fresh restore of the live database: the self-test, the full suite,
and the screen in a browser — a walk with a typing slip refused in the
database's own words and then saved exactly as typed, a correction, a
removal, a walk held with the server switched off and sent on return,
and phone width. One budburst reading on one block moved that vineyard's
plan dates by about a week and narrowed their uncertainty — the point of
the whole thing. Then on live: applied, the self-test passes there, and
the full suite passes (after one run where an unrelated suite's login
token was judged "issued in the future" — a clock blip of a few seconds;
it passed on three reruns and on the full rerun).

## Where I was wrong

One of my own self-test checks was built wrong (it tripped the
duplicate-reading refusal before the permission one it meant to test);
and the walk first defaulted to the alphabetically first vineyard rather
than the one where nearly all monitoring happens.
