# 2026-10-03 16:15 — The field guide's pictures beside each EL stage

Mike made a one-page "Grapevine EL / BBCH Monitoring Guide" and asked
for it in the growth-stage recording. Of the two ways offered, he chose
a picture beside each stage, "and fix the chart labels".

## The labels

The chart's EL and BBCH numbers do not match the app's list, which is
the NZW Modified E-L system with its BBCH pairs (growth_stage_reference).
The chart's BBCH column follows a different pairing throughout (for
example EL 12 / BBCH 53, where the app has EL 12 / BBCH 15). Mike's
answer was that the app's list wins. So each picture is cut without its
printed label and shown under the app's own number and words, and the
chart's numbers never reach the screen. Pictures that moved stage:

- "EL 5, bud burst, green tip" is EL 4 (budburst; leaf tips visible).
- "EL 33, beginning bunch closure" is EL 32 (bunch closure). In the app,
  EL 33 is berries hard and green.
- The unnumbered "flowering near" picture is EL 18 (cap colour fading).
- "EL 19 … ~10% caps off" is used for EL 19 and for EL 20 (10% caps off).
- Mid and late veraison have no EL number, so they are not used.

That leaves 20 stages with a picture. The printed chart itself is
unchanged: correcting it means remaking it.

## What was built

- `public/growth-stages/el-N.webp`: 20 files, 140 KB in all, cut from the
  chart at its own resolution. Each cut was checked by eye on a contact
  sheet, and the first cuts that caught the chart's round section
  numbers were trimmed.
- `app/lib/stagePictures.ts`: which picture is which stage, and why each
  one moved.
- `app/components/Growth/StagePicture.tsx`: a thumbnail beside the
  chosen stage (tap for a larger one) and "Stages in pictures", a grid of
  all of them.
  - The thumbnail is on the walk form, for "EL stage" and for the EL
    number a share suggests.
  - It is also on the correction form.
  - A stage without a picture shows nothing.
- The service worker keeps all 20 pictures (counted in the built
  `public/sw.js`), so they show with no signal.
- `tests/stage-pictures.test.mjs` checks four things:
  - each file exists, at the size the page reserves;
  - every file is used;
  - every pictured stage is on the app's list;
  - the chart's wrong numbers (EL 5, EL 33) are not used.
- The manual, the chronology, the file directory and open-items are
  updated.
