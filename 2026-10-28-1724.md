# Cellar plan: easier to drag on a phone

Mike liked the drag and count but found the phone screen small. Three changes. A drag held near the top or bottom of the screen now scrolls the plan, so a pallet can go from one end of the cellar to the other on a phone, which it could not before. The stack handle is a full-width strip, and pallet lines are taller while rearranging. Tried on a restored copy at phone size with simulated pointer events. Found on the way: the in-app browser runs no animation frames while it isn't being drawn, so the scroll uses a plain timer. Not tried with a finger.
