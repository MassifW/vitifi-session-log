# 28 October 2026 — a WCD sale, and signing wine back in

Mike: "in 'sign wine out' we need to add a 'WCD sale' category. WCD is wine collective direct and has different pricing. We also need to be able to sign wine back in (returns, cancelled orders, no-shows for tastings etc)".

- **WCD, and a guess avoided.** I was about to give the new reason the same excise treatment as "Sale: trade or distributor in NZ" (home consumption). A quick search first: Wine Collective Direct is described (2021 launch coverage) as a marketplace selling NZ wine to buyers *overseas*, powered by nzwinehome. So it is not taken to be an NZ sale: "Sale: WCD (Wine Collective Direct)" asks the country, as Export does, and its excise treatment is **left not set**, which the excise month names ("not counted either way") rather than counts. Mike to say how WCD wine leaves the CCA (exported, or to a warehouse under bond). Added for the first winery only, the rule `make_winery` uses for its template; a winery made later copies it and can switch it off.
- **Signing in is the existing `stock in` record with a reason.** The 7 Oct pallets migration already said a delivery in covers "wine returned from somewhere"; there was just no wine button. Four reasons of a new kind, `return` (Returned; Order cancelled; Tasting or event: back unopened; Other), on the same `stock_reasons` list, so they are each winery's rows and `make_winery` copies them without changing (it copies `kind`). Using a kind rather than a new column kept `make_winery`, `export_stock_reasons` and the bottling screen's filter unchanged. Wine in must give a return reason; a dry goods delivery still needs none.
- **Against the sign-out it came back from, optionally.** "Sign back in" beside each sign-out under Recent opens the form with that sign-out chosen and its wines still out filled in; "Sign wine in" at the top does the same from scratch, with "None, or not recorded here" for wine that went out before the app. `record_stock` holds both sides: no more back than went out less what came back already (following `event_chain` through corrections), the same place, no earlier day, not a bottling's kept-back bottles; and a sign-out with wine back against it cannot be removed, or corrected to fewer bottles, another place or a later day. A sign-in corrected after its sign-out was corrected names the old version, so the function follows corrections forward to the sign-out as it stands (first draft did not, and its own test would have refused that correction).
- **Excise: shown apart, never netted.** The excise month gains "Signed back in: N bottles, X L (shown apart, not taken off the figures above)". Whether wine back into the CCA reduces an entry is Customs' rule and Mike's call; also on the question list.
- **Other changes**: `record_bottling` refuses a return reason for kept-back bottles (a one-line edit by text replacement that must find its place exactly once); `export_stock` gains "Back in against sign-out" at the end (rewritten in full with the winery join that `20261028160000` had patched in); `verify_stock_sign_in()` (18 checks, rolled back, as a cellar hand); the manual's Stock section (WCD, "How do I sign wine back in?", the excise answer); `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/your-data.md`, `docs/chronology.md`, `docs/bottling-and-stock.md`, `docs/open-items.md`.
