## How the sheets were read, and why
- Each daily block is read by its own header row, never by fixed columns: 2021 has an extra "Other" column (MAG1…, kept as `other_as_written`), 2024 puts COMMENT before TA, and the cap-work column is headed PO in some blocks and JO in others.
- The day is the block's date in column B. Two layout traps found by the parser refusing a tank twice on one day: some 2021 header rows carry the date where "DATE" should be (so headers are found by "Tank" in column C), and 2024's 25 March block has no header row at all (a date in column B starts a new day).
- **Temperatures: headed as written, TEMP = must, CAP = cap.** The brief said "the column headed CAP is the must temperature" (the 2025/2026 rule). I did not apply that literally. The 2025 ruling was that the tank tabs are right and the typed log's headers swapped; in 2021-2024 the typed log and the tank tabs agree (both TEMP then CAP), and the CAP column is the warmer one (2024: higher in 421 of 529 rows; 2021: 161 of 166), while in the 2025 log the column headed CAP was the cooler. Applying the 2025 rule here would have swapped every pre-2025 must and cap. One constant (`MUST_HEADER`) flips it if Mike rules otherwise. Needs his confirmation.
- 2021: the typed log only starts on 15 April. Before that each day was typed into the tank's own tab (from 29 March), so a tank-day comes from the log where it has one, otherwise from the tab's typed (non-formula) cells: 204 tank-days. In 2022-2024 the tabs are formulas over the log (the 2022 SSVC1 tab has a handful of typed early days that the log also has).
- The lot a tank held comes from the vintage's own harvest picks (each names its fermenter; one lot per fermenter in every vintage), not the Code column. The Code column is kept as `code_as_written`.
- The vessel: the active Pisa vessel of that code where VITIFI has one; otherwise `vessel_code` only. Not linked: COWCO1-4, PB9001-3, RB1-2, BRL1-2, the 2021-23 FPG, FP2A-C, BABES, FLOYD, 2024's FPG3, and SS8501/SS8502 (which exist only as Clos Miki tanks, retired by Mike on 27 Oct, and made for 2025's Bannockburn ferments). 1,305 of 2,195 readings have a vessel. None invented: Mike retired naming tanks one by one on 27 Oct.
- As for 2025: comments word for word as notes; only the acid columns become additions; SO2, yeast, DAP and inoculations stay in the comments.
