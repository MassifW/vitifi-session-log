# 19 Oct 2026: Ideas, a place to jot down improvements and problems

- Mike wants anyone signed in to be able to jot down an improvement, change or problem inside the app as it occurs, for him and Claude to review later, rather than in a shared document, so staff trialling the app find it easy.
- A small table of its own rather than an event type: an idea is about the app, not about wine or land, and must be private to its author until a reviewer looks, which the shared event records cannot be. The author's name and the time are set by the database, not the caller.
- Row-level security: anyone with an active staff row can add; you read your own; owners and winemakers read all and set the status (new, planned, done, not doing) and a reply; the author can change or delete their own only while it is new, behind a typed word.
- Screen: an "Ideas" link in the header, prefilled with the screen it was opened from; empty sections hidden; a failed load shown as a failure, not an empty list; the phone's last copy shown, with its time, when there is no signal. Included in the "All records" download and the nightly copy.
