# 1 Nov 2026 — Saving a stock take part way, and what is still to count

**Asked:** "When conducting a stocktake, we need an option to save progress (interruptions or counting over a number of days). It should be made clear to the user what has already been counted and what hasn't." ("Try again": an earlier attempt in this worktree did not get anywhere; nothing of it was on disk.)

**Decided (mine, for Mike to check):**
- **Progress is saved as ordinary counts, dated the day they were counted**, never held back to one day at the end. A count stands from the day it was taken, so a carton signed out between sittings comes off the right day; holding Monday's counts until Wednesday would count them against Wednesday's records.
- **The stock take as a whole is a new, small table** (`stock_takes`: place, began, finished, who began and finished it; one open at a place), so every phone and everyone counting sees the same progress. What has been counted is not stored with it: it is worked out from the counts at that place since it began (`countedIn`, `stockMath.ts`), so counts saved on the cellar plan count too, and a removed count stops counting.
- **Typing not yet saved is kept on the phone** (localStorage, per place and wine/dry goods), filled back in on reopening, with "Clear them". Guarded with try/catch; a phone that keeps nothing still works.

- Form: each line "Counted (figure) on (day)", "Typed, not saved yet" or "Not counted yet"; "Still to count (K)" / "All (M)"; buttons "Save N counts" and "Save and carry on later" when none is under way, "Save progress" and "Finish the stock take" when one is (finishing with wines uncounted says how many first: "Finish anyway" / "Keep counting").
