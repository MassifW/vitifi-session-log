# 2026-10-05 12:26 — Growth walks: one stage list per parcel, a block in nine sections, walk order, EL beside BBCH

After walking both vineyards with the app, the owner asked for:
- the walk's separate stage buttons (budburst, flowering, lag phase, first
  berry colour, veraison) to go into the one stage list;
- the younger rows and a pergola added to what a walk lists;
- one block monitored in the nine sections it is picked in at harvest:
  top, middle and bottom, by east, centre and west;
- BBCH numbers beside EL numbers, for people used to BBCH;
- a fish fertiliser's unit set to litres, with another fish product
  (Biomarinus) to replace it in this season's plan.

Later, he'd also like the app to suggest the growth stage from a photo.

## One list on the walk

- **Each parcel chooses its own stage from one list:** the EL stages in
  order, with the five named stages placed among them where the season
  meets each.
  - Budburst, flowering and veraison ask how far they have got, as a
    share.
  - Lag phase and first berry colour are "seen" by being chosen.
- **Parcels can be at different stages on one walk.** Young rows and old
  often are.
- What is stored didn't change: the database always took a stage per
  reading. Corrections use the same list.

## EL beside BBCH

- **Both numbers on every row**, where a BBCH number is known. Which
  comes first is a choice on the Growth tab, kept on the phone and shared
  with the season plan.
- **The six stages without a BBCH number were looked up, not guessed.**
  - The BBCH monograph numbers leaf stages continuously, so three can be
    filled.
  - The other three fall between BBCH stages, or have none (over-ripe).
  - Nothing is added until the owner agrees, as he asked.

## Which parcels a walk lists, and the sections

- **A walk order.** Each vineyard sets which parcels a walk lists first,
  and in what order. It is data, not code.
- **The nine sections are ordinary parcels.**
  - A reading on one feeds the season plan through the larger parcels it
    lies within.
  - A photo's place can name one, and a harvest pick can go on one.
  - Each is drawn once, from the row lines over a third of each row's
    length. They are stored so they are not redrawn as whole rows.
- **Clipped to the block's own boundary.** Unclipped, one corner section
  reached outside the block and lay only 81% within the vineyard's whole
  parcel. The season plan only borrows readings from parcels 90% or more
  within. Clipped, all nine are fully within.

## The fish product

- The four plan items that used it now name the replacement, at the same
