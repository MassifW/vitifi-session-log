# 2 November 2026, 23:30 NZ: the second winery made on live

The shared system's first second winery now exists on live. It is a grower who is also one of the winery's clients, made as a vineyard only
(the growing and vineyard tabs, no cellar), with its manager invited as its owner. The client's name and the person's details stay in the private log.

**How:** the owner ran `scripts/make_winery.mjs --start vineyard` from the project folder. It makes the login as an invitation, then the winery,
then the owner's staff row, and prints a one-time link that the owner sends on. Read-only checks on live afterwards: two wineries, the new one
with exactly one active owner and the vineyard-only module choice. The tenancy and winery-creation self-checks, both rolled back, were clean.

**What went wrong on the way, and was caught:** the walkthrough put the command, with a placeholder email, in a block that the app's Run button
re-sent unchanged, three times. Each run stopped at the script's "Go ahead?" and was answered no, and live was checked after each: nothing was
made. The fix is to ask for the real values before showing a runnable command. Separately, `gh` was not on the terminal's PATH, so the
"no backup running" check was done from the assistant's side.

their own ferments.
