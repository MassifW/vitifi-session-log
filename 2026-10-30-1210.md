# 30 Oct 2026 — Sales by wine: one wine worked by hand, and a design

- The ask: VITIFI cannot yet analyse a wine's sales: sold against sampled, at what price tier, through which stream (export, domestic, direct to consumer), and the share at each.
- Worked through for one wine by hand, from the winery's stock spreadsheet, its accounting system's sales invoices, and VITIFI's own records, all read only. The figures went to the owner and are kept out of every log.
- What it showed about the data: the stock sheet has every bottle that left, with a note precise enough to sort into streams, and its rows add up exactly to its own totals; invoice lines carry item code, quantity, unit price and a home-currency amount, so export prices convert themselves. The gaps: one channel's invoices carry no item code, a distributor is invoiced as one sum a month, and website and card-reader sales have no invoice. The two sources do not fully agree, which is left for the owner to check; a sales screen should show such gaps, not hide them.
- The design (`docs/sales-by-wine.md` in the private repository; nothing built): every sign-out reason gets a stream; the spreadsheet's history comes in as dated sign-outs once the owner says so; prices are read from the accounting system, never typed; tiers are a price list per wine, and a sale at a price on no list is shown as its own price; a "Where it went" view per wine, with revenue visible to owners only, enforced in row-level security.
- Docs only; tests, typecheck and build pass.
