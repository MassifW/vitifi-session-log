# 2026-10-27 22:00 — Track recording through a locked screen, row metres, Floor Work's live map

The owner asked: apps like Strava record a track whether or not the phone screen is on, and keeping a phone on and uninterrupted for a whole spray round is unlikely; can the recorder be improved, and put on the Floor Work tab too? Then, while it was being built: the row metres sprayed matter more than the distance (headlands, trips back to refill); some rigs spray 2 or 4 rows at once, which the recorder can be told before starting; and would it fill gaps, or record for a few hours with the screen off?

**The answer to the last: no, to both.** A web page gets no positions while the screen is locked, on an iPhone or Android; Strava is a native app, which the phone lets use location in the background. Gaps are never filled: a filled gap is how a missed row would be hidden. For hours with the screen off there are two routes: a GPS logger app whose file is attached afterwards (works today), or VITIFI as an installed app with background location (a project of its own; the owner's decision).

**Found while reading the code:** changing tab inside the app also stopped a recording, since each tab unmounts; and gaps were joined up with a straight line on the live map and on saved tracks, though the code's comments said they never would be.

**Built (not released yet, no database change):**
- One recording for the whole app, not per screen. It picks itself up when the app is back in front, after the phone discards and reloads the page within the hour, and when positions stall for 20 seconds with the page in front. It is written to the phone every few seconds and at once when the page is hidden.
- A bar on every tab while a recording exists, with a way back to it; and a "black screen" that keeps the screen on but dark (little battery on most phones, nothing to pocket-tap), held for a second to leave.
- The same recorder with its live aerial map on Floor Work and the Spray Diary.
- Gaps: each gap starts a new GPX segment, the live map leaves it bare with a hollow dot at each end, saved tracks draw it as a gap, and the row-matching never counts a pass across one. Gaps are described as a start time and a length ("9:48 pm for 31 s").
- Row metres: the analysis now reports metres along the alleys (each alley once), row metres (each row once, however often passed) and the totals for the blocks driven in, live under the map and on saved tracks. Each pass of a spray reaches as many rows as the rig sprays, chosen before starting; that number is saved in the track's own file, and a logger's file, which cannot know it, is counted as 2.

**Checked:** the test suite (new tests for segments and breaks, a break ending a pass, row metres for 1, 2 and 4 rows with shared rows counted once), typecheck and build; and in a browser on a throwaway page with a fake GPS driving rows: the stalled-watch restart, the gap drawn as two stretches, recording carrying on with the panel closed, the black screen ignoring a tap and leaving on a hold, a reload resuming the recording by itself, a failed save keeping it on the phone.

**My mistakes:** the first test of the break used a sideways jump that the old code already handled, so it proved nothing; replaced by one the old code gets wrong. A manual line quoted a question as if it were a button label and failed the manual's own test.

**Not tried:** a real phone; the live row metres against real rows; the full app shell in a browser.
