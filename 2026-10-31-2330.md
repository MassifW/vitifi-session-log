# 31 Oct 2026 — Each vineyard's property boundary

_Filtered copy: the vineyard's own boundary and block figures are left out (block layouts); the rest is as in the private entry._

**Asked (from the safety-map session, for Mike):** build a property boundary for each vineyard, kept as that vineyard's own data,
and show it on the Map. In New Zealand from LINZ's NZ Primary Parcels by tapping the land titles; elsewhere, or as a correction,
drawn. Mike that day: "we hadn't considered property boundaries, we should." Not in this piece: lines and areas (power lines,
waterways) and the printed safety map.

**Decided** (`docs/decisions.md`, "Each vineyard's property boundary"):
- A small table, `property_boundaries`, one row per vineyard, not columns on `sites`: its own provenance and notes, one row to
  delete, its own export file, and `sites` (read by nearly every screen) does not carry thousands of LINZ corners.
- A multipolygon, made whole and joined in the database on a 0.1 mm grid, whatever the app sends, so the line is the property's
  edge and not the lines between titles.
- Read by everyone signed in; set, changed and deleted by owners and winemakers (`can_manage_reference`), as blocks are. Asking
  LINZ is checked the same way on the server, so the key is not anyone's to spend.
- Called "Property boundary" everywhere; a test fails on "parcel" in the screens' words.

**LINZ, read from its own pages, not guessed:** layer 50772 "NZ Primary Parcels" (CC BY 4.0, NZGD2000 EPSG:4167, multipolygon;
fields id, appellation, titles, parcel_intent, land_district, calc_area, ...). WFS 2.0 `GetFeature` on `layer-50772` with
`outputFormat=json&srsName=EPSG:4326` and a `cql_filter`: `Intersects(shape,POINT(lat lon))` and `bbox(shape,s,w,n,e)`, latitude
first (LINZ's "WFS spatial filtering" guide: y/x for EPSG:4167 in WFS 2.0), and `id IN (...)`. All three tried with the app's
`LINZ_API_KEY` at LINZ's own example point in Dunedin: HTTP 200, GeoJSON with longitude first. The Spatial Query API, which still
answers 403 for this key, is not needed.

**Built** (branch `mike/property-boundary`):
- `20261107100000_property_boundaries.sql`: the table (`winery_id`, `tenant_fill`, the restrictive wall, policies written
  `(select ...)`, column grants so who set it is the database's), a check trigger (the vineyard is the winery's; the shape made
  whole and joined; 0.01 to 100,000 ha; at most 100,000 corners; a boundary from LINZ names its titles, a drawn one none),
  `export_property_boundaries`, and `verify_property_boundaries()` (28 checks, rolled back).
- `app/lib/propertyBoundary.ts` (asking LINZ, reading its answer, a title in words, the checks before saving),
  `propertyBoundaryActions.ts` (reads, the LINZ questions, save from titles, save drawn, delete). Saving from titles reads the
  ticked titles from LINZ again, so the shapes kept are LINZ's, not the browser's.
- Map: `Map/usePropertyBoundaries.ts` draws each boundary as a dashed cream line on a charcoal edge, under the block outlines;
  an unseen 16 px line takes the tap and opens what it is (area, titles, notes, who set it); "Property boundary" in the legend
  only when there is one; LINZ's credit in the attribution while a boundary from titles is drawn; a failed load is said on the
  map; with no signal, the phone's last copy, said so.
- Drawing screen: the Map's "Draw block geometry" button is now "Draw", and its panel offers "Block geometry" or "Property
  boundary". `Map/PropertyBoundaryEditor.tsx`: choose the vineyard (the map goes to it), "Tap the land titles" (a tap fetches the
  title under it and those within about 400 m; tap to tick and untick; the list with "take off") or "Draw it" (corners, "Finish
  this piece", "Undo the last corner", snapping to blocks, titles and the boundary as it stands); notes; a warning naming the
  blocks left outside; "Save the property boundary" / "Replace the property boundary"; "Delete the property boundary…" with the
  typed word.
- Records and documents: `property-boundaries.csv` (export manifest and the Drive copy's "Vineyards and blocks" workbook),
  `tenancy-tables.json`, `docs/your-data.md`, `docs/services.md` (the LINZ layer and query), `docs/file-directory.md`,
  `docs/chronology.md`, the manual, the tips, `docs/open-items.md`.

**Found and fixed on the way:**
- A drawn boundary in two pieces, the second snapped onto the first one's corners, saved as **two** pieces. Snapping put the
  corner back from its whole pixel, up to half a pixel off, so the shared edge did not meet. Fixed twice over: `snapOnShape`
  (`app/lib/snap.ts`) puts a snap on the corner or edge itself, and the database joins on a 1e-9 degree grid. Redrawn after: one
  piece. The block drawing's own snap has the same half-pixel rounding; left as it is (open items).
- The line was 2.5 px wide and could not be tapped; an unseen 16 px line now takes the tap. In the drawing screen the line takes
  no tap, so a corner can be placed on it.
- `jsonb_typeof(null) <> 'number'` is null, not true, so a title with no parcel id passed the check; the harness caught it.
- A user-facing error said "land parcels"; now "land titles".
