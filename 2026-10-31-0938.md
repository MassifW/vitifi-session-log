# 31 Oct 2026 — New workers read and sign before they arrive

**Asked (Mike):** temporary vineyard hands, sometimes seasonal and sometimes for a day or two, should be sent a bare-bones version of the health and safety policies by a WhatsApp, text or email link from the app, and read and sign it before they arrive.

**Decided (his answers):**
- A personal link with no login, built like the visitor QR page. A new public database function is a security design, so it needed his yes first; he gave it.
- Sent from his own phone: the app writes the message and opens WhatsApp, Messages or Mail. No outside messaging service.
- A one-page starter, and the in-person induction still done on the first day.
- Then his list of what a vineyard worker's induction must hold: the policy short version, amenities, emergency, vineyard hazards with a map, reporting incidents, safety meetings, who to report to, the right to stop unsafe work, visitors, hygiene and sickness. With no repetition.
- Asked, he chose: one page under those ten headings; a short vineyard induction form for day one; the map shown on the link too; incidents reported to the H&S lead or an owner (a no-login incident report is not built).

**Built** (on a branch):
- **The database:**
  - A table of links, with a separate table holding only each token's SHA-256, which nobody can read.
  - One function for staff to make a link, and two the public key can call: one shows the page to the holder of a live link, one takes their signature once.
  - The signed record's copy of the form is built in SQL from fixed text, and a test compares it with the app's own.
  - A link is for one person, works once, and stops after 14 days or when stopped.
  - A library document can now carry one picture (the hazard map). It travels with the page to the link holder, so no file storage is opened to the public.
- **The screens:**
  - Compliance > New workers: make a link, then WhatsApp (the app itself, not the web address, as the spray round sheet already does), text, email, Share or Copy. The list shows sent, opened, signed, stopped or run out.
  - The worker's page, outside the sign-in gate.
  - The library editor gains the picture and a "Send it to new workers" tick.
- **Two forms:** "New worker: read and signed before starting" (one tick, so the page isn't repeated back as rules) and "Vineyard worker induction". The induction has the ten headings; the page sits beside two of its ticks, and the incident report's questions open beside the reporting tick. It notes when the worker signed beforehand.
