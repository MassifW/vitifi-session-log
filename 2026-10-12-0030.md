# 12 October 2026, small hours: LiDAR, survey photos fitted, Senseen exports

- **LiDAR:** the open NZ Elevation data has no 1 m LiDAR tile at either vineyard (checked every regional survey's tile list and the national mosaic's tile table). Only an 8 m national surface covers both.
- **2004 conductivity survey photos:** phone photographs of the paper maps were fitted to the app's block outlines and warped onto a true-north frame (lat/long-aligned rather than NZTM, which is a few degrees off north here), then packaged as map layers. Fit about 5 to 15 m. An automatic brute-force fit never finished and was abandoned; a colour threshold used for transparency broke on dark photos and was replaced by an outline mask. Which depth each photo shows is tentative.
