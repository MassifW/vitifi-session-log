# 2026-10-27 11:00 — Barrel inventory: what the cellar walk changed

The winery's barrel inventory was walked and confirmed against the app's records. About half the barrels in the app were not found, and the decision was to retire those rather than guess which history belongs to which physical barrel.

Built and found:
- A barrel-history workbook from the barrel logs of six vintages and the movement ledger: wine per vintage, logged age, best-estimate first-use vintage. Most can only be given as "no later than", because a log's fill number counts uses, not years.
- Old log names for one barrel often differ by a suffix or one letter; a loose match found a dozen plausible merges, none confirmed.
- A migration to retire the unconfirmed barrels is written and not applied; it refuses to run if its count is off or any of them holds wine.
- Excel had turned some barrel codes into dates; those entries exist in the app and are retired with the rest.

Left for later: running and testing the migration, merging confirmed same-barrel records, storing first-use vintage, and a new four-digit stamped code scheme.
