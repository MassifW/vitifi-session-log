- Mocked up on the map's own aerial imagery (Esri World Imagery) at two
  points: bright green vine rows with roads, and dark bush with dry
  tussock. Four treatments of a shape being drawn, beside a finished
  boundary in the map's green: violet (now); cream with a racing-green
  edge; gold with a charcoal edge; mist blue with a charcoal edge.
- **Suggested: cream with a racing-green edge.** It is the logo's own
  pairing, a light line with a dark edge shows on every background tried,
  and it borrows no colour that already means something — neither the
  state colours (amber, green, red) nor the map's own (boundaries green,
  not yet drawn amber, selected red). Gold disappears on dry tussock;
  mist blue is weaker on pale ground.
