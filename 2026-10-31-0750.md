# 31 Oct 2026 — The first real frost, and no alarm seen

- **The owner asked why VITIFI raised no frost alarm** when the vineyard's own logger (read through Harvest) touched −0.1 °C this morning.
- **It did raise one, twice.** The logger is pulled every five minutes, and the readings matched Harvest's exactly. The alert is set at 2 °C. VITIFI sent "Frost" at 1.4 °C just after 6 am and "Colder" at 0.2 °C an hour later. Apple's and Google's push services accepted both for both of the owner's devices. There was no third at −0.1, by design: another alert comes only a full degree colder than the last.
- **The gap is between the push service and the screen,** which VITIFI cannot see. The likely causes are a Sleep focus at 6 am, the app's notifications being turned down on the phone, or the laptop's browser being closed (a message is held for an hour, then dropped).
- **A test notification was sent** to the phone with the same signing key as live. It showed, but with no sound. The app asks for no silence, so the sound is set on the phone: the app's own sound setting, the silent switch, or a Focus.
- **The limit that stays:** on an iPhone, a web app's notification cannot sound through the silent switch, or through a Focus it isn't allowed through. Only a native app's critical alert, or a call or text from a contact marked for Emergency Bypass, can do that. So VITIFI's alert can't be relied on to wake anyone, and Harvest's alarm stays the one that does. A call or text alarm would mean a new paid service, which is the owner's decision.
- **Also found:** the logger's readings for about twenty minutes arrived late, all at once, so the first alert came that much after the line was crossed. The second vineyard has no alert temperature set yet.
- **Worth deciding:** the notification names the district, not the vineyard; a "send a test" button would let anyone check their own phone at any time.
- No code or database change.
