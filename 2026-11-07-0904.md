**Asked (Mike):** "between blends and bottling, we need a planner for ordering dry goods etc. total litres, bottles magnums etc pallets

**Proposed, then built in three parts** (branch `claude/dry-goods-ordering-planner-27683d`, `docs/bottling-and-stock.md` sections 9 and 10):

1. **The planner** (no database change): Bottling, "Plan dry goods", or "Plan" beside a blend in tank. Litres in tank less the expected loss;
   bottles by size (magnums by number, one size "takes the rest"); cases, full pallets and the part pallet; glass (by the delivered pallet),
   closures, labels (none for cleanskin), capsules, cartons, pallets for the bottled wine, layer boards (less those that come with the
   glass), corner boards, wrap, with a spare share on what breaks; against Stock's dry goods on hand, rounded to supplier packs, with an
   order-by date; "Last time" shows last vintage's bottles by size; printed, or copied as an order list by supplier. Packing, suppliers,
   pack sizes and lead times are kept on the phone (localStorage), so not shared between people: saved plans are step 2, not built.
2. **The dry goods register** (migration `20261119100000_dry_goods_register.sql`, **waiting for Mike's yes**): each pallet or box a record
   (`dry goods unit`) as its label names it; Bottling, "Dry goods register", add (a number range "1-12" makes one each), edit, delete.
3. **A bottling uses it:** "Dry goods used" on the bottling form (each dry good, how many, the open pallets and boxes ticked, "finished" on
   the emptied ones); saving signs them out as one "Used at bottling", made with the bottling the way kept-back bottles are, so editing or
   deleting the bottling changes or takes it back. Fill height, pallet configuration and pallets filled are kept. "Form" prints the 2024
   bottling form and dry goods register, with the chemistry before and after and the WSMP and SWNZ numbers (a setting, `wsmp_number` and
   `swnz_number` in `app_settings`, typed once by an owner or winemaker).

**Read from Drive** (Mike's folder): Dry Goods Inventory, the in-house bottling QC / run / register template, the Kahiwi spec sheet, the
labels checklist, the Wine Solutions July 2025 rate schedule and its bottling spec sheet (first skipped because another of Mike's accounts
