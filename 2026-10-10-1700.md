## The migration applied, and its first bug (11 Oct)
- Mike applied 20261010190000. Its own live check then failed one rule of 14: "the current values are the newest version".
  Cause: "newest" was by created_at, and two versions saved in one transaction have the same created_at, so the order fell
  to random ids. My in-memory run had passed by luck (a coin-flip: 18 of 40 runs fail once looped). Fixed in a new
  migration (the applied one stays as applied): the current version is the head of the replacement chain. 40 of 40.
- Lesson: a check run once in a stand-in is a sample of one; loop anything whose answer might depend on ordering.
