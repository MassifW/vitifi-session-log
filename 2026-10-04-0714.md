Mike asked for barrels to be named as the cellar names them: contents,
then free run or press, then barrel code, then barrel type (new, 2nd
fill…). He also asked to drop the "228 L / 228 L" volume, since barrels
are either full or empty. Done:

## The first invitation could not save

- A colleague opened her invitation link on an iPhone and reached
  "Choose your password", but Save said "Auth session missing!".
- The link had worked. The session it created lived only in the
  browser's cookies, and at Save they no longer held it, most likely
  because the phone blocks cookies. Not established which.
- **Reproduced** on a throwaway local Supabase, with links made exactly
  the way the app makes them. Removing the session cookie between opening
  and saving gave her exact message.
- **Fixed:**
  - the link is redeemed by a client that keeps its session in the
    page's memory;
  - the password is saved with that session;
  - only then is the session handed to the cookies and checked.
  - A browser that won't keep it is warned up front, and after saving is
    told how to fix it.
  - Tested locally: cookie lost mid-way, cookies blocked, and the
    reset-type link a second invitation makes. The test setup was torn
    down after.
- **Released at once**, because nobody new could get in. The tests
  passed with every live suite, and all three after-release checks
  passed.
