  can record sprays). It hands the item to the Actual Diary, whose form
  opens filled in: day (today, or a "Sprayed" report's), block, sprayer,
  tanks (the sprayer's own size, as many as the planned water needs; a
  drone as one mix of the block's water), each product's amount per tank

  why; a contractor's report brings their finish time, name and notes.

  (shrunk on the phone, sent to the reading-photos bucket under

  kept by a correction).
- The record names the plan item it carried out (`fulfils_event_id`, a
  column there since the start and unused until now); the item then
  shows "In the diary" with the day.
- No database change. Manual, chronology, file directory, open items.
