# 2026-10-09 20:40 — The weather filled into a spray record

Mike (with a screenshot of the record form's empty Conditions): "when
confirming a spray round, can the weather conditions auto populate? and
also remain editable? they may sometimes be quite different from local
weather data".

## Built and released (`216cd43`, 20:25; live by 20:36)

- `app/lib/sprayWeather.ts`: the spray's hours (from the hour it started
  to the hour it finished; the early morning, said as a guess, until
  times are given), and the weather over them: mean temperature and wind,
  the strongest gust, the wind's prevailing direction (a vector mean, so
  350 and 10 make north).
- `app/lib/sprayWeatherActions.ts`: Open-Meteo's hourly temperature, wind,
  direction and gusts at the vineyard's position (`sites`), for that day
  (the forecast service for the last ~3 months, its archive before).
- The owner's form and a contractor's "Sprayed": the conditions fill in
  and follow the day and times; a field typed in stays as typed; the line
  under them says where they came from and the gusts. No database change.
- The site's own logger is not sending (no Harvest key), so it is not
  used; Open-Meteo is a model, not a measurement, and the screen says so.
