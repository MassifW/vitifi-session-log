# 5 November 2026, 22:02 NZ: the contract clients' earlier vintages

**Asked:** bring the contract-winemaking clients' earlier records into VITIFI, from the Drive, where they sit in much the same spreadsheet
format as the winery's own. Their latest vintage was already in; asked, Mike chose the earlier vintages (2024 and 2025) over that day's edits
to the latest sheets.

**Found:** a folder per client, a subfolder per vintage, each holding the same "Weigh Sheets & Daily ferment log" workbook the winery uses for
its own fruit: a tab per lot with its weighed bins, a daily ferment log, a barrel log and a topping sheet. Two clients shared one workbook in
2024 (copied into both folders), and one ferment log in 2025. Older vintages of one client exist only as written reports, not as records.

**Built:** an import script that writes one database migration: per lot, the weighed picks (each owned by the client, so the wine stays
attributable to them through every later step), the intake chemistry, the daily ferment readings with the organic-certification cleaning tick,
and acid additions. Every total was checked against a figure the sheet itself holds (a schedule table, the ferment log's weight column, or a
note on the tab). Every row names its winery outright: since a second winery joined the system, the database can no longer assume "the only
one".

**Repairs to the sheets, stated in the script:** three tabs that were copies of one pick, untangled from the ferment log's own comments
(fruit split between a destemmed amphora ferment and a pressed one); a date entered day-month swapped (November for April); a tab heading
copied from its neighbour; an intake reading that was clearly a later one; one pick never weighed, kept with its bin count and no weight; a
ferment with no fruit of its own left out, since a lot with no pick would count as the winery's own wine.

**Not done:** barrels, pressing, topping and bottling for those vintages (the barrel logs run on into the following year with racking and
filtering, and come as their own step); the migration is not yet on the live database. Client names and quantities are kept to the private log.
