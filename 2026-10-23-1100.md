# 23 Oct 2026: one spray over several parcels, and a track on a spray

Mike sprayed one vineyard with the tow-and-fert sprayer and boom and found two gaps in the Spray Diary: a record takes one parcel, and there is no way to see which rows are done after a tank refill.

- **Several parcels, the design.** A record has one target area. Options weighed: one record per parcel (breaks "one record per spray event"); a new unnamed area per spray (a data write into the area table from a spray form); the list in the record's own payload. Chose the last: no database change. The area is the sum of the parcels' areas, and with any area missing no rate is shown (never a sum with a gap). The server refuses parcels at different vineyards, and parcels that share ground, since the area would be counted twice.
- **The limit chosen.** The database-side chemical register and trace read only the first parcel; a join table of a record's parcels would fix it and needs the owner's yes.
- **Tracks, the design.** Floor Work already had the recorder, the saving and the drawing, and a track is its own record linked to any events, so there is no second recorder: the recorder takes a locked site and two callbacks, a Leaflet map draws the path over the aerial photo as a band as wide as the boom with a dot and accuracy circle, and a panel joins them. A track is recorded before the spray record exists, so the spray form lists tracks recorded that day and links the ticked ones when it saves.
- **Bug caught:** the recorder's buttons had no `type`, so inside a form "Stop" would have submitted it. And the map's promise chain had its `.catch` too far from its `.then` for the silent-failure test.
- **Verified** on a restored copy of the nightly backup (no `psql` on this Mac, so a small shim through the database container): a spray over three blocks saved and listed with all names and the rate worked out against the total; a scripted feed of positions drew the band along the rows at phone width, saved, was offered in the form, linked and redrawn from the list. Copy torn down afterwards.
- **Not verified:** a real phone's GPS and screen-on limit; the overlap refusal through the form.
