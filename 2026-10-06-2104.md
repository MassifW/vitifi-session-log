## Where I was wrong

- I miscounted the wines on the stock take by one.
- First design stored only a stock take's difference, so stock on hand
  was a running sum. Changed before anything was saved: the latest count
  now anchors it, otherwise removing a sign-out from before a count would
  move the shelf away from what was counted.
- Then a real bug, found in the browser: a correction entered after a
  count took its own entry time, so a sale from before the count came off
  it again (the wine read 12 bottles too few). Fixed: a correction
  takes the entry time of what it replaces; verify_stock checks it. The
  local copy's figures from before the fix were throwaway.
