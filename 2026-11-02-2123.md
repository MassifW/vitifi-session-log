# 2 Nov 2026: stock counts that save themselves, and cancelling a stock take

*Public copy. Nothing withheld: no client names, rates, yields, vine counts, layouts or certification details come into it.*

**Asked (the owner):** autosave the stock count, so counts are not lost when nobody presses "Save progress": the team is used to Google Sheets and not in the habit of saving.

**Decided:** asked one question, whether the first count that saves itself should begin a stock take when none is under way. The answer: yes, but a stock take begun that way (exploring the app, say) must be cancellable without anything being recorded. The rest followed: a count saves a few seconds after you move on from it, never while you are still typing in it, and at once when the form closes or the phone is put away; a count with a difference waits until its reason box has been left too; a count changed after it saved corrects its own record; a spot count is chosen before typing and saved by pressing.

**Built (on a branch; its database change not on live):** the rules of what to send as a plain module (new record, correction of the form's own record, removal when emptied, a recount when the earlier one is still held offline); a hook that runs them (a short wait after moving on, one save at a time, at once on the page being hidden or the form closing); the list and the cellar plan's count without their save-progress buttons; and a database function that cancels a stock take under way and deletes every count saved in it outright, as the caller, refusing a finished one, with a rolled-back test of seven checks.

**Verified on a local restored copy:** the cancel test 7 of 7, and it named the breakage with its key rule removed; in a browser, every path above. Two faults found there and fixed, both now tested: a saved count's row compared itself with the records that already held it, hiding the reason box; and a count saved as the form closed was still in the phone's kept copy, so reopening sent it a second time. The whole suite against the copy, then one failure of mine (a promise chain with no catch), fixed.

**Not tried:** a real phone being swiped away, no signal, two people counting the same place at once.
