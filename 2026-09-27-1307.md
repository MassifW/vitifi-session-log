# 2026-09-27 13:07 — The map's controls, on a phone

*Block layouts, vine counts, yields, input products and rates, client
names and certification details are withheld from this public copy.
This entry is about where the map's buttons sit on a phone, and contains
none of them, so nothing has been cut.*

Mike sent the second queued task mid-way through the offline-app work
(which is decided and under way, and has its own entry to come): stop
the map's controls covering each other at phone widths. Done first,
because it is small and separate.

## Measured before changing anything

Headless Chromium, local stack, bounding boxes of every map control —
zoom buttons, the Base layer box, "Show where I am", Contours, Layers,
the block count, the legend, the no-connection note, **and Leaflet's
attribution line**, which the task did not list but the tile makers
require to stay visible. A control counts as tappable when all five
points (centre and four near the edge) hit it.

At **375, 390 and 414 px** it was worse than the task said:

- the Base layer box (305 of the map's 384 px) covered **"Show where
  I am" and Contours completely** and the top 21 px of Layers.
  "Show where I am" could not be tapped at all (0 of 5 points), Layers
  failed on 1–4 of 5 depending on width and on whether a note showed;
- the block count overlapped the legend (61 × 24 px at 375);
- the legend and the block count both sat over the attribution;
- the legend touched Layers by 2 px (the task's figure — right, and
  the least of it).

**My task text was wrong on one point:** it said 1000–1920 px had no
overlaps. I had only measured 1280 and 1920. At **768 and 1000 px** the
Base layer box covered the same three buttons — below 1024 px the map
is 384 px tall whatever the width. At 1280 px only the block count
touched the attribution.

## What changed (`app/components/VineyardMap.tsx` only)

- **The Base layer chooser is a button that names the base map
  showing** ("Esri World Imagery"), with the choices under it when
  opened. Open by default at 1024 px and up (where the map fills the
  window), folded below that. On a folded-by-default screen, picking a
  base map folds it again. Open, it stops 12 px above the taller of the
  two bottom corners and scrolls if it has more than fits.
- **The block count moved into the legend** it describes. It was moved
  once before (from over the Layers button to under the buttons, earlier
  today), and there it covered the attribution and, on a phone, the
  legend.
- **The bottom corners sit above the attribution line, however tall it
  is.** Its height is measured (ResizeObserver): LINZ's credit is 73
  characters and wraps to two or three lines on a phone, and each
  overlay adds its own. The heights of both bottom corners are measured
  too, so the open chooser knows where to stop.
- **The bottom-left column is kept clear of the buttons**: at most the
  map's width less 5 rem, so the legend wraps instead of reaching them.
- Nothing about what the map draws changed.

## Measured after

At **375, 390, 414, 768, 1000 and 1280 px**, in three states — normal,
with the no-connection note showing, and with the chooser opened:
**no two controls overlap**, attribution included. Zoom in and out, the
base-map button, Layers and "Show where I am" all take 5 of 5 taps at
375, 414 and 1000 px. The base-map button reads "Esri World Imagery"
folded or open. With the soils overlay's credit added, the attribution
wrapped to two lines (34 px) at 375 and 414 px, and everything moved up
clear of it.

One fix of my own along the way: the first version limited the open
chooser by the button column alone. With no signal the Contours button
disappears, the chooser grew by 52 px and ran 28 px over the note — the
measurement caught it; both corners are now measured.

Also mine: once more, a command that stopped the old server matched
its own command line and killed itself (exit 144). No effect on the
repository; the stop now runs as a command of its own.
