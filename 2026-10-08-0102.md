# 8 Oct 2026 -- A register of the winery's own chemicals

Mike asked whether the app could recognise and categorise documents as they are added, and described what an auditor does: pick a bottle and walk the wine's history back or forward, from any point, with every input used along the way registered and documented.

## What was found
- Documents were entirely manual; nothing read a file. There was no register of the winery's own additions, gases and cleaners for a safety data sheet to attach to.
- The winery kept its register in a spreadsheet retyped every year; the same lots repeated, and several inconsistencies a program can catch (an expired pack still listed unopened, a date in the future, a lot written two ways).
- Published standards from the relevant certifiers and the industry body were read (checklists, approved-inputs lists, cleaning rules). Some of their PDFs could not be parsed by the fetching tool at first; they were read from the downloaded files instead. Certification details of the winery and its clients are private-only.

## Decisions
- Cleaning is a dated procedure presumed followed unless stated: no per-use record, no checkbox -- the app is for ease, not friction.
- Register first, reading documents second; the old register is brought in once, checked by Mike before anything reaches the live database.
- A chemical is one record; each delivery of it is a batch whose states (received, opened, emptied, counted, corrected, removed) are only ever added. A batch is an identity so a document or an addition can point at it for ever. A chemical's earlier states are kept by a trigger. Whether an input is approved by a certifier is a recorded fact with the day it was checked, never assumed.

## Built
- A database change (chemicals, batches, history, a recording function, two more document kinds, documents attachable to a chemical or a batch, two more spreadsheet exports, a 33-check test harness), a Chemicals tab (groups, status chips, expiry and safety-data-sheet flags, one-tap Opened and Used up, count, correct, remove, a delivery form), an import generator for the old register, and the manual, chronology and reference documents.

## Verified

The full suite passes except four checks that read the live database, which cannot pass until the change is applied there.
