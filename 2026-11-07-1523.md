# 2026-11-07 15:23 NZ: Timesheets, "Approve all" and "Send the week to" a business

Mike asked whether, once a week's or fortnight's hours are approved, he could approve a group and send them by WhatsApp or email to the client business whose workers they are. (The client's name is withheld here.)

Found: approval was one person and one fortnight at a time; the client's week view had only a CSV download; nothing was sent from the app.

Built (no database change):

- **Approve all** on the Timesheets tab: approves the fortnight for everyone shown with hours and no approval yet, in one upsert that ignores duplicates, so the existing row-level security insert policy decides who may. A second tap names the people and how many lines are still marked to check.
- **Send the week to** a business: the Web Share API with the week's CSV and a plain-text summary, so the phone's share menu offers WhatsApp or Mail. Where a browser cannot share files, the CSV downloads and a `mailto:` draft opens with the summary. The app sends nothing itself and stores no address.

Choices: no stored email for the client (it would need a schema change, and the share menu picks the contact anyway); approval stays by fortnight, as payroll runs, so the weekly report can go before or after approving.

Verified: the test suite (database suites not run in this checkout), typecheck, build; new tests for the summary text and for who "Approve all" takes. Not yet tried in a browser or on a phone.
