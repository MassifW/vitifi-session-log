## The Yield tab

**In the database** (added with the owner's yes, once its tests passed):
- **Three kinds of reading on a parcel**, as observations: bunches counted
  bay by bay, bunches weighed (one by one or together; at the lag phase,
  first colour, just before harvest, or at harvest), and productive vines.
- **A save function.** It runs as the person signed in. A reading sent
  twice lands once. It refuses in plain words what the forecast couldn't
  use, and a second of the same kind on the same parcel and day.
  Corrections and removals are new records.
- **A self-check** that records, corrects and removes as a throwaway
  vineyard hand, then rolls back. It runs against the live database in
  the test suite.
- **Which parcels each vineyard adds up**, as data, checked not to overlap
  so no vine is counted twice.
- **The factors as settings.**
- **Supporting views:** vines per hectare from the plantings, what has
  been picked, and which larger parcels each lies within.

**On the screen:**
- **Each parcel's card**: its forecast, where each figure came from, and
  what it still needs. Where nobody has counted the productive vines, the
  vine map's planted count stands in, marked "productive vines not
  counted, so a forecast may run high". My first wording called it
  productive, and that was wrong.
- **The vineyard's total**, with the range, tonnes per hectare, litres and
  barrels.
- **A reminder to recount the vines** once the vineyard reaches colour.
- **Each parcel's likely lag-phase window**, worked out from when half the
  caps were off: its own reading, or that of the smallest parcel it lies
  within.
- **During harvest**, what has been picked and what is still to come.
- **Entering numbers.** They go in one at a time, or pasted as a list.
  As you go, the screen shows the mean, how sure it is, and how many more
  bays or bunches would make it ±10%.

**Checked**, on a restored copy at phone width as a local test login:
