# 20 Oct 2026: deleting a vessel added by mistake

- A barrel added by mistake could only be retired, not removed. Mike agreed to a typed-word delete for a vessel that nothing in the records names.
- The database function runs as the caller, for the cellar roles, and lets its own links between tables decide what is "named": anything that has held or moved wine, lost wine, had a reading, sample or document, been named on a task, or had its owner changed is refused in plain words and should be retired instead. Tables added later are covered automatically. The cellar ledger is never touched.
- The vessel's screen gets "Delete this vessel…" for cellar logins, behind the typed word. Tried on a restored copy of the newest backup (7 of 7 checks); not on the live site yet.
