# 28 October 2026 — the two vine-position checks that fail on live, and the outline fit's bearing

Asked by the session that released the WCD change: find out why two live-database tests fail on live and on restored copies (a vine-spacing check, and the rolled-back harness for setting rows against the picture, which timed out on live and on a copy placed a row several metres from where it expected), and fix the cause or record a decision with Mike. Nothing on live changed.

(Block layouts and vine counts are left out of this copy: the spacings, bearings, row numbers and vine counts behind the figures below are in the private entry.)

## Found

- **Both checks predate the row fixes.** Since 24-26 October every block with vines on live has several rows set against the aerial picture, and those rows place the whole block. Both checks assumed a block with none.
- **The spacing failure is the fixes, as designed.** The pergola's fixed rows put its vines about 4-6% further apart in the picture than the sheet says, and the derived places reproduce the fixes exactly. The test asserted the sheet's figure.
- **The harness failure:** it takes the block with the most vines, which has fixes of its own; the "row between" it checks is itself a fixed row, so it sits on its own fix, not between the harness's two. And each of its four saves re-placed every vine of that block, which took it past live's limit for a single API call (8 s, `authenticator`'s statement timeout). A restored copy has the same limit through the API (only psql has none); it simply finished in time on the laptop.
- **A real defect behind the spacing check:** with every fix set aside (rolled back), the outline fit (used when a block has fewer than two fixed rows) still did not keep the sheet's spacing: up to 2.4% wide. Its comment says each vine is a spacing from the next "along its row"; the code stepped along the first row's bearing, and took that bearing with `st_azimuth` on longitude and latitude before using it on NZTM metres. Here a degree of longitude is about 0.7 of a degree of latitude on the ground, so the bearing came out from about 3 to 12.5 degrees off, which also slanted each block's line of first vines. The measured stretch matched 1/cos of each block's error to the millimetre.
- **Corrected, the fit agrees with the hand-set fixes much better:** across most blocks the mean distance from the fix-placed vines fell by half or more, and most of the vines that the fit put outside their block's outline now fall inside it. Independent evidence the correction is right.
- **Where the picture and the sheet disagree:** a handful of fixed rows imply 15-43% more than the sheet's spacing, including rows already suspected of having blank cells before their first vine. A picture from directly above can only make a sloping row look shorter, so a wider step means the numbering (or a pick) and the real vines do not match there. Noted for Mike, not acted on.
- Not the cause, for the record: the open item's guess that the spacing failure was the same problem as "starts of rows after blank cells".

## Built

- A migration: the outline fit takes its bearing in NZTM metres and steps each row by the stated spacing along that row itself (so rows that are not quite parallel keep it too). It moves no vine on live today, since every block there is placed by its fixes. The harness now uses the smallest block that has enough rows and long rows, in the winery it works in, sets that block's own fixes aside first (rolled back), and keeps the same ten checks with the same limits, plus an eleventh: with no fixes, the outline fit keeps the stated spacing along each row to the millimetre.
- The spacing test now holds every fixed row to its own fix's step (|B - A| / number of vines between, to 1 mm), a block with no fixed row to the sheet's spacing (within NZTM's own scale, 0.07% here), and the rest to the old broad limit.

## Tried, on a restored copy of the morning's nightly backup

- Before: both failures reproduced exactly. After: the harness 11 of 11 in about 1.1 s (2.65 s before); the whole suite 2,160 of 2,162, none failing, 2 skipped (no LINZ key), all 70 live-database suites run. With every fix removed on the copy, the corrected fit and the new test agree; a fix recorded without re-placing its block is caught. Typecheck and build pass.

- Not timed on live. A duplicate spatial index on vine places makes every re-placing pay twice; worth removing later.
