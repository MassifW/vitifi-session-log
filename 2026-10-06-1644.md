# 2026-10-06 — Compliance and audits: what each scheme asks, and a lens over the record

Mike: "Let's think about compliance/accreditations/audits. In NZ vineyards
and wineries have any or all of the following: WSMP, SWNZ, Biogro,
demeter (I'm sure there's more!). We need to think about how we can
interogate the data that we're recording in order to satisfy the demands
of an auditor whilst making our record keeping and traceability demands
so much easier (this is a fundamental purpose of the app)".

A design session; nothing built. Written up as `docs/compliance.md`.

## What was read

- **SWNZ Standard, version 1, May 2026** (valid 2025/26), read in full from
  nzwine.com's PDF (the web reader could not parse it; extracted locally
  with pypdf). Every mandatory clause for vineyard and winery listed in
  compliance.md with the evidence it needs.
- **Found:** the organic equivalence stream drops the GrapeLink spray
  diary submission, and every vineyard clause except 1.2, 2.3, 3.11b, 4.7,
  5.1-5.3, 5.7, 6.2 (checked by listing the clause numbers in that
  section). The organic verifier audits annually instead. An earlier design note
  assumed the full stream.
- **Found:** SWNZ requires per spray: application method, spray head
  target, trellis type, canopy density, and a primary target *per
  product*. VITIFI's spray record and its auditor's CSV have none of these
  (one "Reason" per spray; checked the export's columns).
- WSMP: MPI's pages did not load for the reader; summarised from MPI's
  search listings (traceability always verified; altered or missing
  records can be critical, reported within 24 h) and the 85% rule (Wine
  Regulations 2021). Lower confidence than SWNZ; the winery's own WSMP is
  asked for.
- Organic: BioGro modules 3, 10, 13 named but not read. A per-hectare
  annual copper limit (BioGro, 2019; the figure is in the private log). Organic Products and Production Act 2023:
  Regulations in force 3 Oct 2025, Organic Standards Regulations bind
  from 31 Mar 2028 (MPI consulted to 22 Sep 2026).
- Demeter: not researched beyond the preparations being events.

## The proposal (docs/compliance.md)

Compliance as a lens over the records already kept: certifications held
(dated, on the area or winery, append-only); each scheme's clauses as
versioned data saying how each is answered (records, document, season
figure, attestation); product status per scheme, dated, checked at
planning, recording and audit (warn, never refuse); trace back and
forward with the 85% check, organic status and mass balance; an Audit
screen per scheme and season (met / needs attention / kept outside
VITIFI / could not check); an audit pack zip from the same export views;
lapse and due-date reminders on the Calendar. Suggested order, starting
with the four missing spray fields. Four questions for Mike.
