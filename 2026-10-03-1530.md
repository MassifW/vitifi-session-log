# 2026-10-03 15:30 — Topping counts the barrels on the round's day

Mike: "fix topping to use barrel volumes at the round's date". This was
open-items' "Topping takes the barrels' volume when the round is entered".

## What changed

- **The database** (`20261003120000_topping_volumes_on_the_day.sql`,
  not yet on live). `record_topping` counted each barrel on the list
  from `vessel_contents_current`, which is what it holds now. It now
  runs that view's own arithmetic (current movements in and out, and
  losses that left a vessel) stopped at the end of the round's day, and
  writes the day into the round as `volumes_as_at`. "End of the day"
  means a racking on the same day counts as already done. Everything
  else is unchanged: the event, the one `top` movement, the refusals
  word for word, and the grants (re-stated after `create or replace`).
- **The screen.** Each barrel now comes with its dated changes, and the
  form reads them as at the round's date (`barrelOn`,
  `app/lib/toppingMath.ts`), so the total shown is the total that will
  be saved. The total names the day when it is not today.
  - Before, a barrel that had emptied since the last round silently
    dropped off its list. It now stays on, shown as "Empty in the records
    that day: counted at its size".
  - "Add barrels" offers the barrels that held wine on that day.
  - The phone's kept copy from before this change lacks the dated
    changes, so it is refused and the screen waits for the server.
- **`verify_topping`** has 20 checks, up from 18. Barrel B3 is now
  filled after the first two rounds, and 100 L leave B2 later still.
  The first round must still count B3 at its size (it was empty on its
  day), and a new round dated between the two events must count B2 at
  228 L and B3 at 200 L.

## Verified how

  `.env.local` in this cloud session); typecheck; build.
- **A stand-in Postgres 16**, built for the purpose. The migrations
  cannot be replayed from scratch: `supabase start` stopped on a 2021
  phenology import naming an area ID only live has. The stand-in has
  only the tables topping touches, with no row-level security.
  - Against the *old* `record_topping` the new checks failed where
    they should: 656 L rather than 684 L, B3 not flagged.
  - Against the new one, all 17 checks the stand-in can judge passed.
  - The other three are about who may record and who can read the
    rounds, which needs live's row-level security.

  `verify_topping`'s comment would have failed the migration on live.
- **Not done:** a walk-through in a browser. The screen needs a database

## Not changed, deliberately

- The two rounds already on live (18 Sep and 2 Oct, entered 2 Oct)
  keep the figures they were saved with: records are append-only. They
  have no `volumes_as_at`, which marks them as counted on the day they
  were entered. "Correct" on either re-counts it on its own day.
- `topping.csv` does not show `volumes_as_at`. Adding it means
  changing an export view, which the nightly restore checks; it can
  wait for the next change to that view.
