# The vintage's litres on the Cellar tab; Year end's tanks and barrels fixed

Mike (12 Nov 2026): "in the app, can i view the total litre in cellar for a given vintage? for example, I'd like to
see 2025 litres (kahiwi & clos miki)". Answer: not as of today. Stock, "Year end" totals litres by vintage but only on a
past year end; the Cellar tab's Blend overview lists blends with no grand total, barrels only, laptop width only. He
said "yes, add it" and sent a screenshot of Year end, 30 June 2026: the tanks and barrels section read "Could not load
... Minified React error #441".

## Found

- #441 is React's production stand-in for "the server action threw" (resolveErrorProd in
  react-server-dom-webpack): the real error is on the server. Live's postgres log has two "canceling statement due to
  statement timeout", 11 Nov 17:10:54 and 17:11:39 UTC, a minute apart (the second is likely "Try again"). Not proved
  that these two are `bulk_wine_on`: timing it on live was refused by the permission check (production read), so this
  rests on the log's timing and on how the function is written.
- `bulk_wine_on` and `bulk_wine_short_on` call `vessel_lot_balance_on(v.id, p_date)` laterally for every vessel; each
  call reads `movement_current`, `movement_line` and `loss_current` again, under row-level security. The 31 Oct session
  saw it work; the vessels and records have grown since, and the 7 Nov migration had already given its *check* 60 s
  after it ran past 8 s once.

## Built (branch `claude/cellar-total-litres-vintage-0d7427`)

- `getVintageLitres(vintage, owner, variety)` in `app/lib/cellarActions.ts`: `vessel_contents_current` above nothing,
  lots of the vintage, every vessel kind (tanks too), filtered by `lotInScope` and `lotInVariety`; a lot not strictly the
  choice's own (mixed owners; no vineyard on record under one vineyard) is counted apart, not added in.
- `VintageTotal` under "Vintage" on the Cellar tab: "N L in the cellar today · N vessels, N L of it in N tanks", and a
  "Not added in" line when there is wine apart. Loads after the barrel list; a failure says "Could not add up the litres
  in the cellar. Try again"; nothing at all shows nothing.
- Migration `20261123100000_bulk_wine_on_one_pass.sql`: `vessel_lot_balance_on_all(p_date)` (the same three sums, grouped
  by vessel, joined to `vessels` so RLS decides as before), `bulk_wine_on` and `bulk_wine_short_on` rewritten on it, and
  `verify_vessel_lot_balance_on_all()` (120 s) comparing it vessel by vessel with the old rule on six days. Runs as the
  caller; closed to the public key.
- Tests: `tests/cellar-vintage-total.test.mjs`; `tests/year-end-stock.test.mjs` points at the new definitions and gains a
  live suite for the new check; the check added to the security-surface list. Manual (Cellar) and
  `docs/bottling-and-stock.md` updated.
