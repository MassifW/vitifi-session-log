# Floor work recording: mulching and cover crop seeding — 14 September 2026

Built recording for two vineyard activities the app had no way to capture: mulching and cover crop seeding. Prompted by a real record needing entry — mulching vine prunings every second row, seeded simultaneously with a cover crop mix, using a Fendt tractor with a front-mount mulcher and a 9-row direct disc seeder.

## Reusability analysis (before building)

Before writing anything, worked out how much of the existing spray-recording feature actually carries over:

- **Fully reusable, unchanged**: the app's core event-log design (any new activity is a new event type against one shared table, not a new database table each time) meant no schema change was needed at all. Area targeting and equipment also needed nothing new — equipment is already a flexible, free-text-typed table, so a mulcher and seeder attachment just became two new equipment entries alongside the existing tractor.
- **Not reusable**: the spray feature's tank-and-dose model, which is built entirely around diluting a product in water and computing a rate per hectare from that. Neither operation fits — mulching has no material input at all, and a cover crop seed mix is a composition (several components as percentages of one total rate) rather than a single product with a dose, plus a seed dressing rate calculated against seed weight rather than area — a genuinely different kind of arithmetic.

## What was built

- Two new recordable activity types, each with its own shape of detail rather than forcing them into the spray format.
- A new "Floor Work" tab, alongside the existing Spray Diary, with separate entry flows for mulching and for cover crop seeding.
- Equipment can now be recorded as more than one item per entry (tractor plus attachment), reusing the same equipment picker and "add new equipment" flow the spray feature already had.
- The cover crop seed mix is entered directly on each record rather than saved as a reusable named recipe — confirmed this blend was a one-off and future blends may well be unique, so a reusable-mix feature isn't built ahead of an actual need for one.
- Backfilled the real record: mulching every second row across the whole Pisa vineyard, seeded at the same time with a 150kg/ha mix (oats, triticale, ryecorn, clover, peas, beans, vetch, sunflower, medic) dressed with Seacliff Organics Next Gen Root Formula at roughly 250g per 100kg of seed.

## Verified

Type-checked clean, the database migration applied and confirmed live, and exercised directly in the browser — both backfilled records display correctly with full detail, and the entry form for a new record opens and behaves as expected.
