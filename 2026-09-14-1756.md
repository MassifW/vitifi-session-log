# Winery-chain import: vessels, harvest picks, pressing — 14 September 2026

Imported the 2025 vintage's field-to-barrel chain — harvest picks, fermenter vessels, and pressing into barrels — from the winery's own weigh sheets and barrel logs into the data model built for it months ago but never populated until now.

## Groundwork before importing anything

Several things needed confirming with Michael directly rather than guessing from the spreadsheets alone: a suspected co-ferment turned out to be on a different fermenter than first remembered, confirmed by weight against the winemaker's own log note rather than assumed from vine counts; a three-letter code in the barrel log (FR/PR/PB) turned out to describe how the wine was made (free run, press run, or a blend of several fermenters), not what kind of vessel it's sitting in; and fermenter capacity turned out to be a genuinely different kind of fact (kg of fruit) from barrel capacity (litres of wine) — never interchangeable, so the database needed a small schema change to hold both properly instead of forcing one into the other. Destination codes in the barrel log resolved to real wine label names, and some lots that don't trace back to a single harvest pick turned out to be press byproducts, not gaps in the data.

For one site, harvest picks were made against informal compass-direction sections in the vineyard rather than any drawn boundary or mapped area — confirmed directly rather than assumed, so those records honestly target the broader area they're known to sit within, with the specific section kept as a label rather than invented as false precision.

## What was built

- A schema adjustment so a fermenter's capacity (a weight fact) and a barrel's capacity (a volume fact) each have their own proper home instead of one column pretending to serve both.
- Every real vessel from both sites' 2025 season imported, along with every harvest pick, and the full chain of pressing events linking fermenters to barrels — including blends, where a single barrel's wine traces back through multiple contributing fermenters with the actual weighted proportion of each.
- Checked the result against itself, not just against row counts: a barrel's derived fill level from its own movement history matches its real physical size exactly, and a blended lot's derived total matches summing its separate barrels exactly.

## Found along the way

- A real duplication bug in the winery's own barrel-tracking spreadsheet: a handful of rows turned out to be sparse re-entries of barrels that already had a full record elsewhere in the same sheet, caught via the sheet's own internal cross-reference rather than assumed from a naming pattern (two other similarly-named entries turned out to be genuine, separate barrels once checked). Fixed once found.
- Two more cases of the same Excel date-auto-correction bug already caught once before this project on a clone name — recovered cleanly from the cell's own formatting rather than guessed.
- A column-layout inconsistency on one fermenter's sheet that would have silently written a text comment into a numeric field, caught by reading each sheet's own column headers rather than trusting a fixed layout assumed from the others.

## Left open, not guessed

Two small gaps remain where the source records simply don't say more: one fermenter has no recorded pressing date at all, and one barrel has no recorded source or type in the barrel log. Both stay as open gaps rather than being filled with an invented answer.
