## What the old sheets were still good for

Reading them, without importing them, settled two questions about the
method.

- **The multiplier.** The forecast turns a bunch's weight at the lag phase
  into its weight at harvest with a multiplier. The vineyard's own records
  showed bunches gaining more than the sheets' figure assumed, and the
  sheets' forecasts had run low. The owner chose a higher figure from this
  season. It is a new settings row, effective from the season's start, so
  earlier seasons keep the figure they were worked out with.
- **The timing.** The lag weighings were taken 42 to 46 days after half the
  caps were off, every year. That is about a week earlier than the
  published window the app was showing (49 to 61 days, Skinkis &
  McLaughlin 2022).
  - A multiplier only holds at the timing it was measured at: weigh later
    and the bunches are heavier, with less growing left.
  - So the timing becomes two settings next to the multiplier, waiting for
    the owner's yes.
  - Until then the app uses the published window and says that the
    vineyard's own timing isn't set.

## The jobs on the calendar

The owner chose calendar reminders as the next part of the yield system,
over sampling plans and harvest planning by wine. Each vineyard gets five
jobs, placed from the growth stages the calendar already predicts (or has
seen):
- count bunches four to six weeks after half the caps are off;
- weigh for the lag phase, in the vineyard's own window;
- weigh at first colour, the week before veraison;
- recount the vines that won't crop, from veraison;
- count and weigh before harvest, five to three weeks before harvest-ripe.

Each job lists the parcels still to do and drops a parcel once its reading
is in. The job disappears when none are left. Recorded counts and
weighings show as done. The jobs also go into the Google Calendar feed, as
"Opens:" and "Closes:" events.

## A correction

Once the timing was live, I checked every job's window against the dates
in the old sheets. I should have done that before the first commit. Two
windows were wrong:
- **The first-colour weighing** sat later than any season's.
- **The pre-harvest one** sat earlier than any season's. I had written it
  from a harvest date that the app's own pick records contradict.

Both have moved, and a test now holds each season's real weighing dates
inside their windows.
