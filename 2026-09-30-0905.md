- **Reads asked in order, and the plan once a season** (same commit).
  Found while checking the screen: Next.js runs a page's server actions
  one at a time (its client queue, confirmed in 16.3.3's source), so the
  "parallel" reads were sequential, and the predicted stages and the
  forecast waited behind the plan. The plan and predicted reads were also
  per range, so every "next week" re-read the same season. Now the order
  is scope, forecast, done, predicted, plan; the plan and predicted stages
  are read per season and kept (on the phone too, a copy per season).
- **Reads shared within a call** (`977a3c1`), `app/lib/sharedReads.ts`.
  The estimation cascade asked the same questions again and again, one
  after another: every parcel's readings, for every parcel of the site,
  once per past season (`siteAverageDate` in `tier3FromHistory`), and
  again per overlapping parcel. That was 2,858 requests for this season's
  37 plan items. Within a call, each parcel's readings are now sliced from
  one read of all of them (460 rows, one request), and the
  overlapping-parcel, parcel-to-site, site-parcels and regional-baseline
  lookups are asked once. Opened by `listSeasonPlanItems`, the calendar's
  two season reads and `estimateDateForElStage`. One call and one person
  only: nothing crosses between logins or outlives the call.

## Found

- **The home screen would have been expensive.** Before shared reads,
  the calendar at live's distance meant about 28 s to show the plan on
  every opening and about 26 s of billed server time. At 1,500 openings a
  month that is roughly 100 of the 300 free credits. After: roughly 6 s
  of server time an opening, very roughly 25 credits a month. That is an
  estimate: live has not been timed.
- **The Season Plan tab on live has probably been taking ~12 s** for the
  same reason. It gets the same speed-up with the next release.
- **BBCH drawings may be reusable, E-L drawings are not.** Search
  summaries say JKI's 2018 BBCH monograph is CC BY 4.0, but it was not
  confirmed on a primary page (the repository sits behind a bot check, and
  JKI's page states no licence). The E-L drawings belong to their
  publishers; the NZW spray schedule's stage list belongs to NZW.
