# 30 Oct 2026 — The stock sheet's history, sorted for import

- The owner said yes to bringing the winery's stock spreadsheet history (every bottle sold or sampled since the first release) into VITIFI as sign-outs, so a wine's sales can be analysed. This reverses an earlier choice to start the records at the first stock take in the app, for history only.
- Two scripts, nothing written to the live database yet:
  - The first reads the spreadsheet through the Sheets connector, sorts each row into a sign-out reason (sale by channel, sample, laboratory, gift), and writes a review workbook for the owner to check. Its output lives outside the repository, because it names customers.
  - The second writes the rows: a dry run by default, once per row however often it is run, and each record marked as imported. It stops if any record is dated on or after the wine's first count in VITIFI, so stock on hand cannot move, and it compares stock on hand before and after.
- What the sheet showed:
  - its rows add up exactly to its own totals;
  - its rows since the app started all match records already in the app;
  - its "Customs correction" rows are stock-take differences, not removals, and are left out;
  - two entries were counted twice in its own totals;
  - a handful of dates had the day and month swapped or the wrong year, and are read by the month they were entered under, with the written date kept.
- Two questions are left to the owner: whether private invoiced sales and samples sent overseas each need a reason of their own.
- The dry run passed against live. The write has not run yet, and waits for the owner's check of the sorting.
