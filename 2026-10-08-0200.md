# 8 Oct 2026, about 02:00 -- The trace: from a wine back to the vines

Mike asked how traceability looked: could a bottled wine be picked from stock and everything about it compiled from the vineyard's spray diary to the bottling, and could the walk start from any point (a barrel) and go back to the start.

## What was found
- No trace existed. The middle of the chain was linked (picks to lots, blends, the volume ledger, ferment readings). The ends were not: bottled wines were linked to no lot, and the actual spray diary began after the finished vintages.

## Built
- A Trace tab (code only, no database change): from a bottled wine, a wine lot or a barrel, a page read in the order things happened: the sprays on the ground the fruit came from with a withholding check, the picks, the ferment, blends, pressing, barrels, analyses and documents, and the bottling. What is not in the records is written under its stage instead of being left out. It reads only, except that an owner or winemaker can say which lot a bottled wine was made from.
- 12 tests, the manual, the decision record, the chronology.

## Verified
- On a local restored copy, in a browser at phone width: a barrel, a lot and a blend traced back through their lots, picks, ferments and pressings; synthetic sprays added locally checked the spray matching and the withholding check. Not verified with a real spray in a real trace.

## Notes
- A first version repeated barrels for each press day and repeated gap lines; both were fixed after the first real trace.
- Next: link the bottled wines to their lots, bring in the earlier spray diaries, link additions to the chemical batches, then run the trace forwards.

## Later: the finished 2024 blends
- The finished 2024 blends were read from the barrel log's tabs and the bottling forms and recorded as lots with their fruit lots in litres; the stock wines of that vintage were linked to them, so a trace from a bottle reaches back to the picks and barrels. Mike confirmed which tab was which. Wines from earlier vintages have no lots in the app and the trace says so.
