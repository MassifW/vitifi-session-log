# 2026-10-04 13:32 — The season's new spray programme in the Season Plan (built, not yet live)

The owner had reworked this season's spray programme in a spreadsheet and
asked what could be brought into the app. Partway through he added that
a spray dose can be seen "per litre, hectare or row metre".

## The programme, against the app

- **The spreadsheet.** Numbered decision windows by growth stage, each
  with:
  - a default;
  - what to do at low, rising and high risk;
  - how, when and in what conditions to apply it;
  - what to scout for.

  It also has a separate nutrition column, decision rules, a soil
  programme, and a shopping list worked out by hand from rates, passes
  and each site's area.
- **The app's plan for the season.** Last season's sprays, copied
  forward. The new programme does away with much of that routine
  (fewer, decided sprays rather than a fixed calendar).

## What the app could not hold, and now can

- **Doses were stored only per 100 L of mix.** Most of the programme is
  stated per hectare. Turning one into the other needs a water rate, so a
  round sprayed in more water than assumed would have been announced at
  more product per hectare than the label allows.
  - A planned dose is now stated per hectare or per 100 L, as the label
    states it.
  - The list shows it that way, then the other way at the round's water
    rate, and per row metre from the vineyard's own rows.
  - The arithmetic has its own tested module.
- **A water rate per round.** Otherwise the sprayer's usual rate is
  used, and the list says which. The spreadsheet keeps "the same litres
  per row metre" from one vineyard to the next, so the vineyard with
  closer rows gets more water per hectare. Both are shown per row metre,
  so the match can be seen.
- **Rounds and decisions.** A plan item can carry its round number and
  name, and what to do at low, rising and high risk, in the programme's
  own words.
  - They are shown with the app's go, wait and stop marks and names,
    never colour alone.
  - The printed round sheet carries the round's name. It leaves the
    decision out, because by then it has been made.
  - The calendar calls items by their round.
- **Reserve stock.** A product held in reserve can be held for more than
  one application. One "nutrition held in reserve" item per vineyard
  replaces nine, which would each have put two entries on the calendar.
- **The shopping list.** "What this plan needs" adds up each product for
  the season, planned and held in reserve, by vineyard. Anything it
  cannot work out is listed as missing, never as nothing.
- **Taking an item off the plan.** It was never possible before.
  - "Remove from plan" records a removal with its reason; nothing is
    deleted.
  - Removed items are listed on the screen and in the download.
  - The plan download now also gives each planned dose as stated.
- **A revision used to overwrite the item's notes with its reason.** It
  now keeps the notes and records the reason beside them.

  Three not yet chosen are left out and named in the notes.
