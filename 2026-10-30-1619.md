# 30 Oct 2026 — Find my row, made accurate

- The owner said the Vines tab's "Find my row" was "working very poorly" for position. It asks the phone where it is and names the row and vine the person is standing at.
- Four read-only investigations (the live data, the phone's position, the map's frame, the code) found the vines' modelled places were good where they could be checked: within about 0.3 m of a surveyed row design in one block, and of a phone track in another. The faults were elsewhere:
  - **The phone's first position was used.** A single browser request is answered with the first position the phone hands over, which can be kept from earlier or taken from a mast. A track recorded in the app began 11 m out and took over 20 seconds to come within a metre.
  - **The nearest vine named the row.** Someone standing in an alley is half a row from the rows on both sides, so even a perfect position was a coin toss between them; where a dead vine left a gap, it picked the next row. With an honest 3 m position the named row was right well under half the time.
  - A phone's accuracy figure is a radius the truth is inside about two times in three, not the 95% the web standard says.
  - The aerial the rows were placed on is in the national datum as of 2000; a phone gives today's position, about a metre north-west of it here because the land has moved. Not corrected in code, since it depends on which picture each block was set against.
- Built (no database change): the phone is watched until its position settles (kept, coarse and approximate positions refused, walking or a locked screen starts the wait again); each row becomes a line, and the answer is which two rows you are between, near which vine, with the range of rows you are likely in when the phone is more than a row out. The rows' lines are kept on the phone, so it works with no signal after the first time. "I'm at this vine" measures the phone against a vine you can name and moves later finds nearby for ten minutes.
- Four independent reviewers, each finding checked by a skeptic, confirmed twelve problems, all fixed: two neighbouring blocks claiming the same strip, ragged and dead row ends called "outside", one coarse first reading ending the find, a phone clock that disagrees with its fixes, a locked screen, and others.
- Checked: the full test suite, typecheck and build; the database suites against a restored copy of live; on a snapshot of the real vine places, every vine's own place comes back as its own row and vine, and a point between two rows names those two rows everywhere; in a browser on the restored copy with a simulated phone, the right alley and vine were named and the block switched by itself.
- Not yet checked: a real phone in a real row, and how long the "I'm at this vine" correction holds. A few row placements look worth a second look on the picture. Photo captions still name the nearest vine's row (changing that needs a database change).
