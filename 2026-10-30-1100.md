# 30 Oct 2026 — Pisa weather from a neighbour's station; the disk-full outage

**Built:** Pisa's weather from a neighbour's Harvest station (Torr Estate, site 7966; the owner agrees to our using and storing it). Station row on live with Mike's yes; key `HARVEST_PISA_API_KEY` in `.env.local` and Netlify. A sensor in `trace_map` can name its units (`{ trace, from: 'm/s' }`). `scripts/load_harvest_api_history.mjs` reads a station's whole history through the API, 200 readings a request, stepping over gaps. Mike's choice of storage: every reading October to April (frost can come any time in the growing season; "mid summer frosts have happened"), one row a New Zealand day May to September ("daily data is sufficient").

**Loaded:** 787,150 rows from 4 Oct 2017: Diamond Block temperature (Mike's pick over Middle Block), rain, average wind and gusts. No humidity sensor. Soil moisture and soil temperature not taken.

**Wrong, and what it cost:** Harvest's sensor list says the anemometer is m/s, but `get_data` returns km/h; I converted again, so wind was 3.6 times high. I fixed it with one UPDATE over all 787,000 rows, which filled live's disk (then 2 GB) at 20:38 UTC; the database crash-looped for about 20 minutes until Mike raised the disk to 8 GB. My own watch of the outage was broken (a malformed URL) and showed nothing useful. The fix was redone in batches with VACUUM between. Three gusts near 489 km/h blanked as glitches. CLAUDE.md now carries the rule this broke (another session's PR #14): measure, batch about 20,000, Mike's yes with rows and MB.

**Thinned with Mike's yes:** the neighbour logged every minute through 2024 (366,479 rows), which took the 2024 weather export past the time limit and would have stopped tonight's readable records. Thinned to every 10 minutes, keeping every reading at 2 °C or colder: 331,566 deleted in 17 batches of 20,000, no backup running, db 1,134 MB and WAL 944 MB throughout. Export test passes against live.

**Also:** filled Bannockburn's 75-minute gap of 28 Oct (7 readings). Rewrote `skipPlannedSprays`' call so the schema test stops reading its argument's keys as arguments (another session's code, false alarm). A permission rule for the loader was added by Mike.

**Verified:** full suite against live 2,231/2,232 (the one false alarm, then fixed and its test passes), typecheck, build.

**Released** `88829b2` (Mike pushed production). check:live, check:auth, check:bundle pass. First Pisa pull 21:45 UTC; gap since the history filled (244 readings).
