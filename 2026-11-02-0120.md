# 2 Nov 2026: Vines fit a phone again, the Season chart's moon table removed, reading a graph under the finger, the toilet and the hand-wash apart

2. "There's still a whole section on the moon and frost on the season tab. We can remove this."
3. "run a finger over the graph and see the records for that moment", as Harvest and the phone's weather app do, on the weather and season screens.
4. "Our toilets and handwashing are not always in the exact same location, we need to be able to add them either together or separately and keep them on the map sent out to new staff."

**Done, three branches, three draft PRs, nothing released:**
- **PR 57** (`mike/vines-width-season-moon`, no migration).
  - The cause of the Vines fault: the 30 Oct change put the zoom buttons at the picture's corner by wrapping the scrolling box in a new grid item. A grid item is as wide as its content unless told otherwise, and the old scroller had been exempt because it scrolled. So the canvas's width became the page's, and the phone zoomed the page out. `min-w-0` on the wrapper fixes it.
  - The Season chart's "Frost nights and the moon" table is gone, with the phase in a frost day's tooltip and the manual's paragraph. The Forecast's moon under each day stays. `tallyByPhase` and `MOON_SHAPE` in `app/lib/moon.ts` are now used only by its test; I left them in.
- **PR 58** (`mike/chart-scrub`, no migration). `app/components/useChartScrub.ts`, used by the Season chart and the Forecast's hourly graph.
  - **How it works:** a finger held still for 0.22 s takes hold of the graph, and sliding moves the reading. The touchmove listener is not passive, so the slide is prevented from scrolling once held. A move of more than 8 px before the hold cancels it, so a swipe still scrolls. A tap or click reads that column, and a mouse reads where it points. The reading stays where it was left.
  - **What it shows:** a line above each graph, with a line drawn down through the graph.
    - Season: low and high, rain, ET0, soil water, pressure-bomb readings, and each lane's work.
    - Hourly: temperature, sky, rain, wind and its compass direction, gusts.
- **PR 59** (`mike/toilet-handwash-apart`). **Its migration `20261109120000_map_toilet_handwash.sql` is NOT ON LIVE and waits for Mike's yes.**
  - "Toilet and hand-wash" is now "Toilet". "Hand-wash" is a new safety icon on the same dark square: in Popcorn a tap, a teardrop and cupped hands; in Standard a tap over a basin.
  - **Together or apart:** together is one point with both icons (a point already holds one or more); apart is a point each.
  - **The migration:**
    - redefines `map_point_kinds()` (sorts renumbered from 7);
    - adds `hand_wash` to the points showing `toilet`, two on live (read-only count), so they keep meaning both;
    - leaves the export view alone.
  - **The map for new staff:** the printed safety map and its phone picture draw every point and key every icon shown, so the hand-wash appears with no change there.
  - `tests/map-points.test.mjs` now reads the latest definition of `map_point_kinds()`, not the first migration's.
