# 31 Oct 2026 — The compliance maps redrawn

**Asked (Mike):** make the health and safety maps more attractive and intuitive: the two vineyards' site maps, the wider route map, and the winery's layout.

**Found first:** the winery's own documents disagreed about one vineyard's assembly point (three different places) and its street number. Mike settled both before anything was drawn, since a map has to pick one.

**I was wrong this morning:** a page I imported to the app's library came from the repository's copies of the library texts, which were stale. The live library had already been corrected by hand. Only that new page was wrong on live; it was corrected, and the repository copies now match.

**Built** (files, not app code):
- Four A3 maps, as PDF and JPEG, saved beside the originals in the winery's Drive folder (the originals untouched): each vineyard's site safety map, a "getting in and out" map from the state highway, and the winery floor plan.
- **What the vineyard maps are drawn from:** LINZ's aerial basemap (CC BY 4.0) in place of screenshots of a commercial map service, VITIFI's block outlines read from the database, and the winery's emergency plans and hazard registers for the content.
- **How they look:** the brand header, ISO-style safety symbols, labelled callouts, a north arrow and scale. Each has a side panel: what to do in an emergency, where things are, hazards, and the nearest medical centre.
- **The winery floor plan** is a vector redraw of the winery's own H&S layout. That layout turned out to be to scale: its room areas match the building's fire report within a few percent.
- **A phone version** of both vineyard maps is the picture on the new-worker page in the app.
- **Tools:** a small Node script using `sharp` (SVG drawn over the aerial photo), with a minimal PDF writer around each image. The script holds one winery's layout, so it stays out of the app's code.

**To check on site:** the positions of gates, toilets and sheds (read from the old maps and the photo), and the winery's exits, which aren't drawn: the plan says to follow the exit signs.
