# 2026-09-30 07:08 — EL stage on the walk; a whole-vineyard outline fixed; photos next

*Block layouts, vine counts, yields, input products and rates, client
names and certification details are withheld from this public copy.
Here that means block names, areas and vine numbers.*

Mike found two points of the growth-stage handoff confusing. Reworded,
he answered both: a block missing from one vineyard's "whole vineyard"
outline should be in it, and the walk should record the EL stage itself.
He also wants photos to become the record of each stage, "for continuity
and removing human error". Both database changes applied to live with his
yes; not released.

## Found

- **One vineyard's whole-vineyard outline left out a block.** Its area and
  vine figures were right all along (they came from the spray diary's own
  whole-vineyard row, which includes that block), so nothing recorded
  against it was wrong. Only the outline was: it is built from whole-block
  shapes, and the missing block is the only one drawn in two halves, which
  a whole-block shape cannot find.
- **One of those halves is itself drawn too small** — a good part of its
  vines lie outside it — so one parcel still sits only partly inside the
  whole-vineyard outline until someone redraws it on the map.
- **The plan's strongest evidence (an observed EL number) was looked for
  only on the plan's own parcel**, never on the blocks within it — so an
  EL stage recorded on a block could not reach a whole-vineyard plan item.

## Where I was wrong

I first told Mike the whole-vineyard area left out that block and that
per-hectare figures were inflated by about a quarter. They weren't; the
area includes it. Corrected the same turn. And the fix's first self-check
claimed more than the fix could do (every parcel inside), refused on live,
and rolled itself back — the claim was wrong, the check was right.

## Built

- **The outline fixed**: both halves added. Its check now asserts what is
  true: the whole-vineyard outline covers each of the block's parcels at
  least as far as the block's own outline does.
- **"EL stage" on the walk**: pick the stage each parcel has reached from
  the E-L list, with its description, for the weeks between the named
  stages. The database requires the number and refuses a share.
- **The plan borrows EL readings from the blocks within a whole-vineyard
  parcel**, as it already did for budburst and flowering.
- The list, the correction form and the parcel card show EL readings by
  number and description.

## Verified

On a fresh restore of live: both changes, the self-test (22 checks), the
full suite, and in a browser — one budburst reading on the previously
missing block now moves that vineyard's plan dates, and one EL 12 reading
on a block turned the plan's EL 12 item into an observed date. Then both
applied to live. The full suite passes against live.
