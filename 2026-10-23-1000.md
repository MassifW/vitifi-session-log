# 23 Oct 2026: the moon's phase on the weather forecast

Mike: "can we place lunar phases on the weather forecast (particularly with respect to frosts)". Asked him two things before building; his answers: the moon on the forecast and the Season chart, and a frost watch at a low of 2 C or below with light wind. He added that the Bannockburn vineyard should eventually get its own model from forecast against measured temperatures, with clear sky, light wind and perhaps the moon.

- **Read first.** The ten-day table and hourly graph are `ForecastDetail.tsx`; the Season chart's "Notable weather" strip is `SeasonChart.tsx` with `notableDays` in `seasonChart.ts` (frost: a daily low of 0 C or below, 1 September to 15 May). The hourly forecast arrives by its own read (`getHourlyForecast`); the moon needs none.
- **The phase, `app/lib/moon.ts`.** Worked from the date: the new, first-quarter, full and last-quarter moments from Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms* chapter 49 (main terms, no planetary terms, so a few minutes out), then each day placed by how far it is through its stretch. A key day is the New Zealand calendar day the moment falls on; other days are crescent or gibbous, waxing or waning, with the lit share at about 3 am. Tested against dates from eclipse tables (2024-04-08, 2025-09-07, 2026-01-18, 2026-02-17, 2026-03-03, 2026-08-12, 2026-08-28) to within 10 minutes (about 2 minutes in practice), and for order and spacing across a whole year.
- **The frost watch, `app/lib/frostNight.ts`.** A night (midnight to 7 am) of 2 C or below: "watch" if at least 75% of its hours are clear or mostly clear (WMO 0 or 1) and the average wind is under 10 km/h; otherwise a cold night, saying cloud or wind. A missing sky figure is never counted as clear. These lines are my reading of "clear and still", not Mike's; they are plain numbers at the top of the file.
- **Screens.** Ten-day table: a moon line under each day (key phases bold), then the frost line. Hourly graph: a moon at each day's head. Season chart: the phase in each frost day's tooltip and a "Frost nights and the moon" table under the key (frost nights against nights, per phase), saying plainly that the moon does not cause frost and that a few frosts are too few to show a pattern. `MoonIcon.tsx` draws the lit part in gold on a charcoal disc and every picture is also named.
- **Caught on the way:** the first drawing had the terminator the wrong way round for gibbous and crescent moons (a 87% moon drew as a sliver); seen in the browser, fixed, and the second try read correctly. The first layout put the moon beside the day name and squeezed the temperature bar on a phone, so it moved to its own line.
- **Verified:** unit checks (`tests/moon.test.mjs`, 20) and a temporary page with made-up figures at 375 px wide (removed). **Not verified:** the frost-watch line in a browser (it needs the real hourly forecast, i.e. a login), the graph's moons, and anything on a real phone.
- **Not built, for Mike to decide:** the vineyard model. The forecast is not kept day by day, so there is nothing to compare measured lows against; it needs a table for forecasts (a database change, his yes) and some months of nights before it says anything. The Season tally already works on the season's own temperatures.
