### Second round: a functional palette

Mike: "The brand colours can remain restrained and elegant. But when the
interface needs to communicate state, urgency or action, clarity should
win." His four, kept apart from the brand colours:

    Information Blue  #35657A   planned, scheduled, information
    Forecast Amber    #8C5A14   predicted, provisional, wait, warnings
    Success Green     #276749   done, confirmed, saved, go
    Warning Red       #A73C32   stop, errors, immediate attention

"That way the red doesn't suddenly become a VITIFI brand colour; it is
simply an interface signal colour."

- Each is the 700 step of a Tailwind scale; the other steps sit at the
  lightness Tailwind's own blue, amber, green and red have at that step,
  so every place that used those colours keeps its contrast. They replace
  the stock colours everywhere in the app. Actions stay brand green.
- **Never colour alone** (his words: "the distinction should survive
  colour blindness, poor displays and sunlight in the vineyard"). Each
  state has a shape: planned a dot, predicted a diamond, done a tick; stop,
  wait and go (octagon, pause, play) defined for later. The calendar's
  chips are a pale fill with the colour's border and mark; entries carry
  the colour down their left edge and the mark before their kind; the
  month view shows the marks.
- Left alone on purpose: the map's own colours, chosen to show on an
  aerial photo, where dark green would vanish.
- A test keeps the colours, the icon files and the offline page in step,
  holds every state to its own shape, and fails if a stock colour class
  comes back.
