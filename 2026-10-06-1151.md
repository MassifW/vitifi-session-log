## Heat units between stages

Mike: "We have traditionally based some forecasts on numbers of days
between events. These may be more accurate when viewed as number of heat
units between events ... apply it where we can. If you can find other
indicators in the research, please make a note of these."

`scripts/heat_unit_calibration.mjs`: every stage reading on live, each
site's daily temperatures from Open-Meteo's archive, leave-one-season-out
per step. Degree days over 10 C. Out by (days, RMSE):

    budburst -> flowering   day count 6.4  heat 2.7  heat with rest at normal 3.9
    flowering -> lag        2.5            6.6       3.0
    lag -> first colour     1.5            6.9       3.9
    flowering -> veraison   5.9            7.5       4.8

BEDD-style capping at 19 C: no difference. GFV/GSR-style (base 0 from 29
Aug, season-wide): budburst 3.1 vs 6.1, flowering 2.8 vs 3.8, veraison 3.2
vs 3.6, but only with the season's temperatures known -- not yet tested
with normals. Applied: budburst -> flowering only, 166 (145-197) heat
units. `app/lib/dailyHeat.ts` (recorded from the archive, then the forecast
model's past days, then 16 days' forecast, then the date's normal from
earlier seasons); `phenologyActions.ts` counts heat where the link has it,
and falls back to the day count naming why.
