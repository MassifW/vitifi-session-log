# 2026-11-09 17:54 — Dry goods planner: the open pallet

Mike, after the release of PRs 91 to 93, on the pallet count under each wine in the planner: "something that
would be useful would be to know the number of bottles predicted on the open pallet (# of whole pallets plus 1x open
pallet)".

Built: `palletWords(fills, specOf)` in `app/lib/dryGoodsPlan.ts` turns a wine's fills into "N pallets: so many full and an
open one of so many × 750 ml". Each size stacks on its own pallets (as the count already did), so a wine bottled in two sizes can
have an open pallet of each: an open pallet of magnums and one of 750s. No open pallet: just
"2 pallets". The count itself is unchanged; `stack()` already worked out `partBottles` and it was never shown here.

The worked example in the test is one wine as planned on Mike's screen: a magnum run that fills exactly one pallet,
and the 750s some full pallets and an open one.

Tests: two new in `tests/dry-goods-plan.test.mjs`. Manual line updated. Branch `claude/open-pallet`, PR to follow, no
migration. Not looked at on a phone.
