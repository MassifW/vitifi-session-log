# Dry goods planner: on hand by name, one line of closures

Mike (10 Nov 2026, screenshot of the planner's Dry goods table, every On hand "--"): "The dry goods planner hasn't auto
filled with numbers on hand. The closures for magnums and 750ml bottles are the same."

Found: On hand was never automatic. Each line had to be linked to an item on Stock's dry goods list by tapping it, and
that link is kept on the phone (localStorage), so Mike's laptop had none. Stock's dry goods list on live today:
two kinds of screwcap, gift boxes, magnum and 750 six-packs, cellar trays and their wires, corner boards and pack
dividers (quantities left out here). No glass, pallets, layer
boards or wrap at all.

Built:
- `stockMatches(key, items)` in `app/lib/dryGoodsPlan.ts`: the stock items a line's name fits (closures: screwcap,
  closure, cork; glass by its size; cartons by size; pallets not wrap or boards; and so on). One fit is taken and the
  screen says so ("Corner boards, by its name"); several are left to choose ("2 on Stock could be it: choose"); none
  stays "--". A line someone chose "Not on the list" for stays unlinked.
- Setting "Magnums take the same closure as the 750s", on by default and for a phone's older copy: the magnums' closures
  go on the 750s' line, keyed `closure:750` so a stock item already chosen for it still applies, named
  "Closures, 750 ml and Magnum (1.5 L)".

Left as it is: which screwcap a run uses (matt or Kahiwi) is Mike's choice, once per phone. The link is still per
phone; keeping it for the winery would need a setting in the database.

Tests: new in `tests/dry-goods-plan.test.mjs` (closures, and matching against the live list as it is today); the old
test that expected a separate magnum closures line now expects them on the 750s'. Manual updated. Not seen in a browser:
the restored copy from 9 Nov was torn down.

phone; it is now kept for the winery in the app's settings table (no migration). Precedence: the winery's choice, then
the phone's own, then the one name that fits. A login that cannot write settings keeps its choice on the phone and is
told so.
