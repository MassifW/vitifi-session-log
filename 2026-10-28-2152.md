# 28 October 2026, 21:52 — Filling a trace's gaps

Mike traced SI24 Scordatura Icosa 2024 from the bottle and found two "Not in the records" lines that were wrong in substance: two picks with no block, and no pick for BBWBMIX or DADA. BBWBMIX is a press-blend barrel (2617) of the two ferments BBWB#1 (2517, SSVC13) and BBWB#2 (MYDANAJM, SSVC14); DADA is the 2023 wine carried forward, half a barrel (Cadus) of which went in. He asked for it fixed, and for a way to fix such things in the app himself.

## Found
- The ledger already held BBWBMIX's making (its pressing names both ferments) and DADA 2023's (DABLE + DA115). The trace only followed `lot_parentage`, so it stopped. Fixed in code: a lot with no recorded blend follows its movements' source lots.
- DADA 2024 was opened as stock in two barrels with no link to DADA 2023. Linked on live (Mike: "fix data"). A query for any other lot with the same pattern (same name in an earlier vintage, no source, no pick) found none.
- No pick before 2025 has a block. The 2024 ferment sheets name the ground in their own codes, which do not map one to one onto the app's Hill parcels, so I did not guess them.
- No ferment readings exist for any vintage before 2025 (Mike had forgotten; he sent the wine folder for all vintages). That is an import of its own.
- The recorded pressing dates for BBWB#1/#2 are the pick dates. The sheets say the presses were about 27-28 Apr and 25 Apr 2024. Not changed.

## Built
- `traceActions.ts`: ledger-derived parents ("was made from"), and `fixes` on the trace (lots with no source, picks with no block, links said by hand); `addLotSource`, `removeLotSource`, `setPickParcel`, `listPickParcels`.
- `TraceTab.tsx`: a "Fill a gap" panel under the trace, by role (cellar: source; vineyard: block; owner/winemaker: take back, typed word).
- `20261029150000_lot_source_take_back.sql`: owners and winemakers may delete a link on a lot not made by a blend in the app. Not on live.
- Manual entry; `tests/trace.test.mjs` updated (the trace now writes four things, never the ledger).
