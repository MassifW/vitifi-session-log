# 2 Nov 2026: a crew link for a group's WhatsApp, and "Change…" where a draft says it is a draft

**Asked:**
1. A neighbouring vineyard will trial VITIFI. The two share staff: its crew comes to us one or two days a week, starting tomorrow. "We'd like to send them a health and safety induction link to their whatsapp group from which the individual employees in the group can digitally sign before arriving on site."
2. With a screenshot of the new-worker page showing Draft: "it wasn't apparent to me how I could mark the document as current".
3. Speed limit signs on the map, and notes on hazards such as access roads slippery when wet.

**Decided:** the live new-worker link is one per person. Of per-person links today, a group link built, or both, the group link.

**Built (on a branch; its migration not yet on live):**
- A crew link: one link for a group, each person signing for themselves with their own name, up to a number staff choose (2 to 50). Each signature is its own row and its own compliance record, so the list, the first-day induction and the exports treat each person as one.
- No new function for the public key: the two the new-worker link already used learn the second kind. A crew link opens with no name, says when it is full, and treats the same name twice as one signature.
- The export gains two columns, placed before the record id every export ends in, so the view is dropped and made again.
- Staff: "Make a crew link for a group", a message asking each to sign for themselves, WhatsApp opening to choose the group, and a list with "n of m signed" and each name under it.
- Worker: "This link is shared with your crew", "Sign for someone else on this phone", and a plain answer to a repeated name.
- A draft document's notice now carries the "Change…" button: it was only at the foot of a long page.

**Verified:** on a local copy restored from last night's backup: the migration applies, the new harness 18 of 18, the old one still 36 of 36, the full suite 2,646 of 2,646 with every database suite. The suite's first run failed twice, both from my own mistake: the new export columns came after the record id. It was caught before live, as that test was written to do. In a browser on the copy: a draft marked current through the new button, a crew link made, signed on a phone-sized page, a repeated name answered, the staff list counting it.

**Speed limits and slippery roads:** both possible today, with the winery's own icons (a speed sign is a drawing from the library on the warning plate, the limit in its name) and a hazard icon's name and extra line. A proper round speed sign with its number is offered as a later change.

**Then:** the migration applied to live by Mike, with no backup running; on live the harnesses and the full suite pass.

**Released** the same morning, with the owner's word to merge without the second reviewer. The after-release checks pass. One of them first failed because my own local build had been made against the local copy, not the live settings; rebuilt, it passed.

**Not done:** the page marked current on live. A second winery in VITIFI of its own needs the shared system for many wineries, designed but not built.
