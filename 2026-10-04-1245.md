# 2026-10-04 12:45 — More contrast, a shaded icon; released with Documents

The owner liked the new look but wanted more contrast and more "pop",
including in the phone icon. He suggested gold, icy blue or orange, and
later asked to bring back the subtle shine the first icon proposal had.

## Measured before choosing

- The icon's cream on racing green is already 11:1. It wasn't short of
  contrast but of colour: deep green and off-white are both quiet.
- Bright accents are strong on the green and very weak on cream
  (1.1–2.4:1). So the spark belongs on green surfaces only.
- The real flat spot was white cards on a cream page, at 1.09:1.
- Five leaf colours were shown on light and dark phone wallpapers. Orange
  was advised against because it would blur the amber "predicted" and
  red "stop" states; lime because it reads as "done".

## Built and released

- **Racing gold** on the icon's leaf and the selected tab's underline.
  Never used for state, never used as text on light backgrounds.
- **The icon's green gently shaded**, the icon only; buttons and bars
  stay flat. Each copy of the icon on a page gets its own gradient name,
  so one can't lose its shading.
- **Cards that stand off the page:**
  - a slightly deeper cream page behind them;
  - a thin warm edge built into the shadow of the cards that had only a
    shadow;
  - input boxes left alone, since a light edge would make them too faint
    to see.
- **Small grey text darkened** to stay above 4.5:1 on the new page.
- Colours checked from the real page, since the browser panel was
  hidden. Tests now hold the new rules, including the minimum text
  contrast.
- Released together with the Documents feature, as one release instead
  of two. All post-release checks passed, and the live site serves the
  new icon.
