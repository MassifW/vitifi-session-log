# 24 Oct 2026, evening: the vine spreadsheets into VITIFI: answers, cleaning, the table, the load, a read-only Vines tab

(Block layouts, vine counts and clone-level counts are kept in the private log; the figures below are left out on purpose.)

- **The owner's answers recorded:** a cell with two entries means the later entry is the vine there now; a duplicated entry is one entry; a note about a tag found in the soil is kept as a note; unknown clones stay unknown, including a batch of transplants that were mixed up; one irrigation naming scheme at one vineyard is defunct and is not imported; two stray count notes and the hand-watering tag are dropped. Harvest is usually late March (an earlier remark of mine that tied a date to harvest was wrong).
- **Order changed:** the owner would rather enter the data in the app than on the old sheets, so recording comes first and is done in the field on a phone, walking the rows: vines layered in August and not yet recorded, replacement plantings, then the recount of dead and non-productive vines.
- **Irrigation zones:** two zones that are not real zones are to be retired, and two blocks that share one subsurface system become one zone, with the older in-line line getting its own zone. Written as a migration.
- **Found:** a parser for both workbooks already existed, so the importer builds on it; the cleaning rules are a small tested module; a duplicate check had missed a batch of cells because it only compared rootstocks.
- **Built:** a `vine_plant` table (one row per physical vine in one place, carrying its own address because the app treats the existing `vines` table as the living vines and reads its gaps as vacancies), a download file for it, and an idempotent loader that is a dry run by default.
- **Tried on a restored copy of the nightly backup, twice,** before anything touched the real database. The copy showed that the first design would have changed vacancy and harvest-provenance reads, so it was redesigned first. Copies were removed afterwards.
- **Applied to the real database with the owner's yes** (he ran the push and the load): read back and matching.
- **A read-only Vines tab** (alpha): a block at a time laid out like the sheets, coloured by any of several layers, with a key that counts, a card for one vine, and a go-to-row-and-vine box. Checked in a browser on a restored copy and on a phone-sized view; a vine read from the screen matches its cell on the sheet.
- **Gates:** the full test suite (all that can run without the live database), typecheck and build pass.
- **Not checked:** a real phone, the live data signed in, no-signal use, and the live-database suites.
