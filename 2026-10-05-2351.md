# 2026-10-05 23:51 — Five seasons of yield sheets, read and checked, waiting for a yes

The owner pointed me at his yield monitoring history: "the indices file".
It turned out to be a Drive folder with a sub-folder for each season, each
holding that season's yield workbook. One vineyard's go back to 2021,
the other's to 2023. The app was quit partway through, and I continued
when asked.

## What the workbooks hold, and what they don't

- **Kept every season:** weighings of about a hundred bunches at the lag
  phase and at first colour, weighings just before harvest, bunch counts
  after each thinning pass, the productive vine count, and the harvest.
- **Never filled in:** the harvest bunch weight tab.
- **Copied forward from year to year, which matters for an import:**
  - one later workbook's sample tabs are the previous year's, dates and
    all;
  - some date cells are last year's;
  - one tab's harvest figure belongs to another vineyard and year;
  - "total" columns are averages of the other columns, worked out by
    formula.

## The import

A script reads every workbook and reports everything it leaves out, and
why:
- **Only what was typed in.** Formula columns are never imported, so
  nothing is counted twice.
- **Dates only from the sheets, and only inside their own season.** A
  date outside it is a leftover copy. Where a reading has no date of its
  own, it takes one from the same pass elsewhere in the sheet, and its
  note says so. A reading with no date anywhere is left out and listed.
- **One pass is one reading.** My first version split a two-day weighing
  into two readings. The forecast would then have used the second day's
  small fragment as "the latest", so I fixed it.
- **The app's parcels.** Sub-blocks are pooled into the parcels; a sample
  the sheet used for two parcels is recorded on each, saying so; rows
  with no parcel today are left out.
- **Each reading carries a reference built from what it is,** so the
  import cannot land twice.

The result is 193 readings. I first wrote the totals into the decision
record by hand, and got them wrong. I corrected them from the script's own
count before committing.

## Checked

- **On a restored copy of the live database:**
  - all 193 landed, and a second run was refused;

  - where a pre-harvest weighing exists, the app uses it as weighed (as

- **A new test** reads the workbooks and checks the same things,
  including that forecast. All tests pass.
- **Not applied to the live database.** That waits for the owner's yes.
