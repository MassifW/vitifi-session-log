# 9 November 2026: a topping round fills its barrels

**Asked (Mike):** "before the release. please check: all barrels and elevage vessels (with the exception of tanks and VC's) should be
presumed full unless otherwise stated. Barrels hold 228litres. I recall having seen some recorded as 223. This may have been fixed
already but worth a check." Then, to the plan: "Yes, plan it", "Yes, build it".

**Found (live, read only):** 58 barrels recorded a few litres short of their size, all from the 10 March 2026 racking (assumed
fills, 5 L of lees each; the 198s from three 600 L barrels). The screens already showed them full (a barrel topped since its last filling
counts full), so PRs 91 and 92 were not affected. The ledger was not: `record_topping` took the topping wine out of its keg and put it
nowhere. That day's three rounds took exactly the shortfall. Also: a vessel coded KEGS is recorded holding more
than its capacity (probably wrong; not changed).

**Built** (branch `claude/topping-fills-barrels`; migration `20261122100000_topping_fills_barrels.sql`, waiting for Mike's push):
- a new movement kind `top_up`: each barrel on a round takes its share, first what the records hold it short of its size, then (shared by
  size) what evaporated since, recorded as an `evaporation` loss of its own wine; too little leaves it short. No movement line: the round's
  `top` movement already drew the wine from the keg, so lot by lot it nets to nothing. `lot_barrelled_down_litres` leaves `top_up` out.
- `record_topping` from live's own text (confirmed identical on a restored copy): edits and deletes take the shares and losses with the
  round and refuse one that would leave a barrel short of wine a later step drew out (`topping_barrels_check`).
- the three recorded rounds are filled in the order recorded.
- the app: a barrel is known by its own wine, not the topping wine in it (name, blend call, whose wine), with its litres counting all of
  it (`app/lib/toppingWine.ts`, `cellarActions.ts`); topping wine of another vintage in a barrel counts in its litres without listing a keg
  of topping wine under every vintage. The topping screen and manual no longer say "Nothing is recorded against a single barrel".
