## What was built

- `frost_nights`: one row for each logger each night (22:00 to 08:00, named by its morning): the forecast low made
  about a day before, the model's own after-the-fact low (both from Open-Meteo's archive of past forecasts, hourly, at
  the vineyard), and the logger's low. Service role writes; staff read under the usual policies and the winery wall.
- `frost_night_logger_lows()`: each night's logger low summed in the database, service role only. Checked on live
  (read-only) against the week to 17 Nov: 60 readings a night, 0.7 °C on the 17th.
- The weather pull, once an hour, fills missing nights (September to April) a season at a time back to Sep 2024, then
  each morning from 09:00. A night's logger low needs 30 readings (Torr Estate keeps a single daily summary for some
  stretches).
- The offset: the median of forecast less logger over cold nights (logger 3 °C or below), at least 8 of them, worked
  out on each request, never stored.
- The evening push: 18:00 to 18:14 NZ, tonight's lowest forecast hour (22:00 to 08:00) less the offset, at or below the
  station's alert temperature, to the same watchers; kept as kind `likely`; once a night.
- The Forecast tab: the frost watch judged on the corrected low, showing the forecast low beside it, and a line saying
  how many cold nights the offset comes from. No line where there is no offset (empty sections hide); a line when it
  could not be loaded.
- Manual, services (the previous-runs address), file directory, your data, chronology, open items, the design doc.
