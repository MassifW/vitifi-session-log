# Cellar plan: drag to rearrange, count on the plan

Mike asked to reorganise the bottled cellar plan by dragging whole stacks or single pallets, and to enter stocktake counts for open pallets on the map.

Built on the Stock tab's cellar plan: a Rearrange mode (drag a stack by its handle to swap squares, or one pallet onto another square: on top, or just above the pallet dropped on; all moves saved as one new plan record) and a Count on the plan mode (a box per open pallet and dry-goods item; saved as one ordinary stock take, full pallets taken as unchanged, a wine split across squares summed). No database change: both use the existing plan and stock-take functions. The move rule is a plain function with its own test.

Checked: npm test (no live database here, so its suites were not run), typecheck, build. Not tried in a browser or on a phone; finger dragging is the part most likely to need adjusting.
