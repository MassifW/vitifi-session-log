# Pisa flowering spray: a tractor window before rain, planned in the app

Mike (23 Nov 2026): a tractor spray is possible at Pisa tomorrow. Rain is forecast and no good window after it is
certain. Flowering has started. The question was whether to use the pass for something beneficial, or skip it for a
drone pass later in the week.

## What the records showed

The season's Pisa spray records were read from the app: biological, sulphur and nutrition products only, mostly applied
by drone. The last sulphur was two weeks earlier. No active ingredient, resistance group or label rate is entered for
the products used, so the app cannot check rotation or rates for them, and one early-season round is not recorded.
Rates were worked back from past records (the figures stay in the private log). Two zinc records from last season
disagree with each other by a factor of ten, so one is probably an entry error.

## The advice, and where it moved

- The first answer assumed botrytis and downy mildew mattered, which argued for spraying before the rain. Mike said
  neither is an issue at this site. That left powdery mildew cover at flowering and better bunch-zone coverage from the
  tractor as the case for going; a drone pass soon after the rain became a fair alternative.
- The Senseen sensor is not working, so trace elements are a presumption: boron and zinc at flowering for fruit set,
  with petiole samples at full bloom to settle it next time. Calcium, magnesium and seaweed were left out.
- Mike chose sulphur plus a zinc and a boron foliar product.

## Written to the app (Mike's yes)

- A new zinc foliar product added to the product register, pending label confirmation.
- A planned spray for tomorrow at Pisa, with per-tank figures, mixing order, a jar test and the drone fallback in its
  notes. The zinc dose is left blank on purpose until the label rate is entered. Product names and rates are withheld
  from this public copy.

## Uncertain

- The sulphur rate came from drone passes at a lower water rate; if its label is per 100 L, the tractor rate needs
  checking against it.

## Correction (later the same session)

Mike corrected the zinc and boron products to a different brand already in the register. The planned spray was updated;
those two doses are left blank until read off the containers, because no label was found online and last season's
records for them disagree with each other by a factor of ten. The product added earlier is now unused.

## Rates found (later the same session)

The zinc and boron containers carry no rates; they were found in the supplier's brochure, attached to an email from
January, and match what this season's programme already planned. An amino-acid fertiliser left over from an earlier
round was added to use it up. The planned spray now carries all four products with doses (withheld here).

Magnesium was added to the same planned spray at Mike's call.

Calcium was added to the same planned spray at Mike's call, with a jar test for pH (the water is alkaline) and a note that the acid on hand can react with calcium.
