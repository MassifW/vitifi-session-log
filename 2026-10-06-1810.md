## Built

- **Database** (`20261006190000_analyses.sql`): `analytes` (28, data not
  code; unit, decimals, accepted range, 'volume' or 'estimate' blending,
  the names labs use); `events.target_vessel_id` with a check it never
  stands without the wine; `record_analyses` (a round whole or not at all,
  as the caller, idempotent per sample, the wine taken from the ledger on
  the sample's day, an empty barrel refused with what to do, corrections
  and removals superseding); `analyses_current`,
  `analysis_results_current`; `export_analyses` (one row per result) and
  `export_analytes`; `export_observations` without analyses; the
  completeness check reading both; `verify_analyses`, 24 checks.
- **Screens**: Cellar -> Analyse (grid like the barrel log: 3.62, <0.1,
  nd; columns remembered per phone; a wine's barrels in one go, one
  vessel, or a wine as a whole; in-house or laboratory with report and
  sample numbers; recent list with Correct and Remove). AnalysesAbout on
  the barrel, lot, blend and ferment screens: latest, history, the
  weighted figure with "From 3 of 6 barrels, 684 of 1,368 L" and the days,
  "<"/nd counted but never averaged, pH and free SO2 marked estimates,
  barrel by barrel on a blend.
- **Mike's answers changed the list before it went live**: Pacrim plates
