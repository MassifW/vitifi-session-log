# 18 Oct 2026: bringing in the earlier vintages (2023 read first)

- The winery asked for its 2021 to 2023 vintages to be brought into the app, starting with barrels and lots, then ferments and weights, then bottlings and lab results. Only its own wine; contract wine is kept in separate folders and is not part of this. Records of excise registers are left out for now.
- The source files were read straight from the shared Drive folder; each year holds the same set of workbooks as the 2024 vintage did.
- The 2023 barrel log turned out to differ from the 2024 one in ways that matter (its totals rows, how rounds are stacked, a long barrel stay), so it has its own reader. The reader only prints what it finds; nothing has been imported.
- Findings so far: the wine stayed in barrel for about twenty months and was racked into its finished blends and bottled in early 2025; the ferment log carries no press dates; many of the barrels are not yet in the app's vessel list under the codes the log uses, so each needs matching by someone who knows the cellar.
- Not done: no migration written, nothing changed in the app or on the live site; 2021 and 2022 not started.
