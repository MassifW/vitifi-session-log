# 2026-09-29 17:39 — Other wineries, eventually; the 2024 PDFs kept; documents next

*Certification details are one of the six things the public copy
withholds. The certificates copied today are suppliers' quality
certificates for dry goods, described here only by kind and count.*

Mike raised three things: importing the 2024 PDFs and certificates,
uploading documents in the app, and a new user being able to bring their
information in, PDFs included.

## Decided

- **"A new user" means both, eventually**: a new person at this winery,
  and in time other wineries. So VITIFI stays built for one winery, but
  nothing new should make a second one harder: no winery's name, place or
  layout in the code; new kinds of record attached to the records they
  are about, so they belong to whoever owns those; stored file paths
  leaving room for whose a file is. Turning a second winery on — each
  winery's records kept apart in the database, its own settings, and a
  way to bring in its own spreadsheets, maps and documents, where every
  import today is a script — is recorded as its own future project.
- **The 2024 PDFs are kept now**, then uploaded once the app can hold
  documents.
