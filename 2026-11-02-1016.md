# 2 Nov 2026: speed limit signs on the Map

**Asked (Mike, through the session that started this one):** he went to put speed limit signs on the Map and found no icon. He was told he could make his own today (a gauge on the warning triangle, the number in its name) and offered a proper round sign with the number on it. This session built that.

**Decided: one icon per limit, not a number kept on the point.** Four new built-in safety icons after "Hazard": `speed_10`, `speed_15`, `speed_20`, `speed_30`, named "Speed limit 10 km/h" and so on. The number is part of the sign, so it is drawn on the map, named in the Key and printed on the safety map and its phone picture through the paths every icon already takes. Keeping the number in `detail` would have meant parsing free text, and a new column would have touched the table, its grants, the save, the export and the checks. The cost: the limits are fixed (another is a new row in `map_point_kinds()`), and a track with two limits is two points.

**Built, on branch `mike/speed-limit-signs`:**
- `app/lib/mapPoints.ts`: a new plate, `speed` (white disc, red ring), not offered for a winery's own icons (`SIGN_PLATES` unchanged), since the number is the sign. Popcorn: a 3 x 5 pixel number inside a 2-pixel ring. Standard: the ring as a 4-unit red stroke on a circle of radius 14 (the plain circle's 15 would push the ring off the 32 x 32), the digits as line paths on the 24 grid in black, so the print's canvas draws them from the same paths.
- `supabase/migrations/20261109140000_map_speed_limits.sql`: `map_point_kinds()` redefined whole, speed limits at 16 to 19, everything after them four places down. No data change. **NOT ON LIVE; it needs Mike's yes and his `npx supabase db push`, and must be on live before the code is released.**
- `tests/map-points.test.mjs`: the four signs (round, red ring, white inside, black number, distinct, safety, not offered to own icons, the standard plate). The test reading the SQL only accepted letters and underscores in a code; widened to digits, which is what first failed.
- Manual (Map section), `docs/chronology.md`, `docs/open-items.md`.
