# 9 November 2026: expected losses, step by step

**Asked (Mike):** "Here are some typical flows from winemaking. Some loss should be anticipated at each stage. Some wines will have less
movements, some more. Let's ensure that we build in (if we haven't already the ability to anticipate and record movements and losses).
4% per annum is a typical per annum figure for evaporative losses from barrel." Red: kg harvested, litres pressed (and/or free run), tank
to barrel, barrel to barrel (post ML), barrel to blend tank, bottled. White: the same with pressing tank to fermentation tank after the
pressing. Then, to my proposal: "Yes, build it".

**Found first:** recording was already there: every one of those moves is a pressing, racking, blend or bottling in the ledger, and a
loss row (lees, evaporation, heel, sampling, spill, unexplained) can go with each. Nothing anticipated a loss but the yield forecast's
litres per kg and the bottling planner's single 2%. How the records hold loss, read on live (read only): a racking's or blend's litres
are typed the same either side and its lees are loss rows (the share is in the private entry); a bottling's loss is
litres drawn against bottled; pressing to barrel has no loss typed anywhere (71 moves). Most moves carry an assumed figure (a
barrel's nominal fill) on one side, so out-less-in alone would have said nothing; the loss is the larger of out-less-in and the loss rows.

**Built** (branch `claude/expected-losses`; no database change):
- `app/lib/lossChain.ts`: the two flows; each recorded event as a step (by its kind and the vessels at each end: tank to tank, tank to
  barrel, barrel to barrel, barrel to blend tank, bottling; topping, adjustments and bulk out are not steps; an event of mixed steps is
  left out); the winery's own figure per step and colour, with how many moves recorded any loss; litres per kg into barrel; what wine in
  barrel today should come to by a bottling day (4% a year for the days to it, then into the blend tank, then bottling). Tested.
- `app/lib/lossActions.ts`: the reads (paged past 1,000 rows), narrowed to "Whose wine" for the vintage sections; the figures saved as
  an `app_settings` row (`expected_losses`), which owners and winemakers can already write.
- Cellar, "Losses" (`LossChain.tsx`): the expected figures and the records beside them, "Change the figures" for owners and winemakers;
  the vintage step by step against them, moves above expected listed under each step; the wine in barrel by blend, to litres and 750 ml
  bottles, with a total.
- Starting figures: 4% a year is Mike's; the rest are the records rounded, except tank to tank and tank to barrel, which have no loss
  typed and are my guesses.

**Verified:** unit tests (13). On live data through a local dev server under Mike's existing sign-in, reading only (the figures were not
saved; "Change the figures" opened its 12 boxes and was cancelled): with "Kahiwi and Clos Miki", the litres in barrel for 2026 and 2025 (in the private entry, yields)
each equal to the Cellar tab's Blend overview. 2025 first came out one barrel short: a 2024 barrel carried forward into 2025
(Declassified), which the Cellar tab counts; fixed by taking the vintage's barrels from the Cellar tab's own list. Before narrowing to
"Whose wine", it also counted contract clients' barrels; fixed.

**Does not fit:** topping puts back what evaporates from topping wine, so the 4% is lost from the winery's wine but not always from the
barrel's; the projection takes it off the blend, the cautious way. Red or white is read from the variety; anything not known to be
white is followed as red.

**Not yet:** the bottling planner still starts at its own 2% rather than these; the tank steps have no recorded loss to compare with.
