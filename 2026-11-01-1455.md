# 1 Nov 2026 — Counting on the plan over several sittings; a single pallet's count and customs

**Asked (Mike):** "'save and carry on later' only appears in 'stocktake' not in 'count on the plan'", then: "“Save and carry on later”, “Carry on counting” and “Finish the stock take” ... All 3 of these need to be available for both counting input options." And: "Occasionally, it may be useful to count a single pallet or such-like, not necessarily as part of a full stocktake. **Why it was missing:** "Count on the plan" saved through its own call to `recordStock`, so PR 51's stock take buttons, which live in the list form and `saveTake`, never reached it. I should have caught that when building PR 51; its handoff said plan counts count towards a stock take, which was true, but not that the plan could not start, carry on or finish one.

**Built** (branch `claude/plan-count-progress`, from `main` at `943adb1`; no database change):
- `CellarPlan.tsx` saves through the Stock tab's `saveTake`, as the list does: "Save the count" / "Save and carry on later" when no stock take is under way; "Save progress" / "Finish the stock take" (warning first how many wines and dry goods are not counted, here or on the list) when one is.
- Each count box shows a tick (counted in the stock take, on the plan or the list, with the figure and day on hover and to a screen reader) or a dot (still to count), and the count bar's top line says how far it has gone.
- "Carry on counting on the plan": the plan's own button while one is under way, and beside "Carry on counting" on the Stock tab's "under way" line (opens the plan's count and scrolls to it).
- A side effect: the plan's count used to refuse with no signal; it is now held on the phone, as the list's always was.
- `TakeSave` moved to `stockMath.ts` to be shared. Manual, `docs/bottling-and-stock.md`, file-directory, open-items; tests in `tests/stock-take-progress.test.mjs`.

**Gates:** `VITIFI_REQUIRE_LIVE=1 npm test` 2,631 of 2,631 against live (all 80 database suites); typecheck; build. With spot counts: 2,634 of 2,634, typecheck, build. Not looked at in a browser.


**Spot counts (Mike, choosing from three options put to him: "Own record, marked spot (Recommended)"):** a spot count's difference stays a record of its own for the customs adjustment, marked as a spot count -- not carried into the next stock take, and not a check that changes no figure. Built on the same branch:
- Both ways of counting offer "Save the stock take", "Save and carry on later" and "Save as a spot count" when none is under way.
- A stock take saved at once is marked in `stock_takes` as begun and finished that day (`markStockTake`, which checks the day's format before it goes into a query filter, and does nothing when a stock take already covers the day). A spot count is not marked, so `stockTakeDays` calls any counted day no finished stock take covers a spot count (null when the stock takes could not be read).
- The Stock tab card says "Spot count of ..." and the record "Spot count differences", with a line saying what that is; the picker marks spot counts.
- No back-fill was needed: read-only, live had one stock-take day before this (13 Oct 2026, Olivia Ross), and it was dry goods (cellar trays, six-pack cartons), which the customs record leaves out. No wine count from today's stock take is in the records yet.
- Held with no signal, a stock take saved at once cannot be marked, so it would show as a spot count; the note says so.
- Manual: "Can I count just one pallet, not a whole stock take?"; tests for the rule and both screens.
