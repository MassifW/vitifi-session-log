# 1 Nov 2026 — Counting on the plan over several sittings; a single pallet's count and customs

**Asked (Mike):** "'save and carry on later' only appears in 'stocktake' not in 'count on the plan'", then: "“Save and carry on later”, “Carry on counting” and “Finish the stock take” ... All 3 of these need to be available for both counting input options." And: "Occasionally, it may be useful to count a single pallet or such-like, not necessarily as part of a full stocktake. I'd imaging that this is possible now but we should consider the customs reconciliation etc."

**Why it was missing:** "Count on the plan" saved through its own call to `recordStock`, so PR 51's stock take buttons, which live in the list form and `saveTake`, never reached it. I should have caught that when building PR 51; its handoff said plan counts count towards a stock take, which was true, but not that the plan could not start, carry on or finish one.

**Built** (branch `claude/plan-count-progress`, from `main` at `943adb1`; no database change):
- `CellarPlan.tsx` saves through the Stock tab's `saveTake`, as the list does: "Save the count" / "Save and carry on later" when no stock take is under way; "Save progress" / "Finish the stock take" (warning first how many wines and dry goods are not counted, here or on the list) when one is.
- Each count box shows a tick (counted in the stock take, on the plan or the list, with the figure and day on hover and to a screen reader) or a dot (still to count), and the count bar's top line says how far it has gone.
- "Carry on counting on the plan": the plan's own button while one is under way, and beside "Carry on counting" on the Stock tab's "under way" line (opens the plan's count and scrolls to it).
- A side effect: the plan's count used to refuse with no signal; it is now held on the phone, as the list's always was.
- `TakeSave` moved to `stockMath.ts` to be shared. Manual, `docs/bottling-and-stock.md`, file-directory, open-items; tests in `tests/stock-take-progress.test.mjs`.

**Gates:** `VITIFI_REQUIRE_LIVE=1 npm test` 2,631 of 2,631 against live (all 80 database suites); typecheck; build. Not looked at in a browser.

**A single pallet's count (asked, for Mike to decide):** possible now -- "Save the count" or "Save N counts" with only that one filled. It is a finished count at once: stock on hand becomes the count, and its day appears in "Stock take differences" as a record of its own (one wine counted). Options put to Mike in the chat.
