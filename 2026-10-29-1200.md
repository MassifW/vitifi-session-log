# 29 October 2026 — Check and Trace, narrower and shorter

Mike: Check should look only at the vintage and wine chosen, with a way to see
everything; Fill a gap only works when a pick came from one parcel, and most
ferments came from several; Trace's Ferment stage lists every reading and
addition of every ferment, where a link to each ferment sheet and weighted
averages would serve. Apply it broadly.

Built (branch claude/check-trace-improvements-a1be50, no migration):

- Check follows the Cellar's vintage; "All vintages" and "All wines" widen it;
  under one vintage it counts the issues in the others. Volume-gap findings had
  no vintage at all (`vintage: null`), so they now take their lot's.
- Trace, wine of several ferments: Harvest and Ferment open with a summary
  (ferments, kg, days picked, weighted harvest date; Brix, pH, TA, whole bunch
  at intake weighted by kg, each saying what share of the kg it covers). One
  line per ferment with tank, kg, Brix, whole bunch, readings, additions
  counted, and "Ferment sheet" opening FermentSheet. One ferment: unchanged
  apart from the sheet link.
- Fill a gap: tick several blocks per pick (setPickParcels). Stored like a
  multi-parcel spray: payload.target_area_ids + parcel_names, target_area_id
  only when there is exactly one. The vineyard stage checks sprays on each.
  kg is not split between blocks.

Verified: npm test (one failure on my own empty-state wording, fixed), typecheck,
build. Not in a browser: this worktree has no .env.local.

Not fitting: a several-block pick has a null target_area_id, so anything that
reads only that column still treats it as unplaced. pH averaged linearly by kg
is an approximation. CDEASTER's flag says -0L but 1.043 L/kg — the two figures
disagree; not looked into.
