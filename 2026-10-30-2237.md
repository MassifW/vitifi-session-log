# 30 Oct 2026 — Price lists, and each price named by its tier

- **The owner asked where to put the wine prices,** and shared the winery's price sheet. Its lists price a range of wine in a bottle size, one tier at a time (retail, trade, wholesale, staff, export), some tiers including GST and some not.
- **The new table holds price lists, readable only by owners on the row.** Each row is a list, a range (words a wine's name contains, so it covers every vintage), a size, a tier, a price, and whether the price includes GST. No wine is named in code.
- **Naming a price by its tier.** A sale's price without GST is set against each tier's price without GST, within a few cents. Where none fits, two slips are tried: a tier's price charged including GST by mistake, and a tier less a round discount. Exports invoiced in another currency are said as such. Anything else is "not on a price list", which is itself worth knowing.
- **The screens.** A "Prices" page on Stock for owners to keep the lists. On each wine's "What it sold for", every price now carries its tier, and a "By tier" summary adds them up.
- **On the live database** with the owner's approval: the table, then the sheet's prices. The prices are kept out of the code repository, as revenue is, and the tests use made-up ones.
- **Seen against real sales:** most domestic prices were named, including a trade price charged including GST by mistake. The commonest retail price turned out to be on neither list, which the owner can add.
- On a branch, all checks pass, waiting for review and release.
