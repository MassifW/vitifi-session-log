# 22 Oct 2026: compliance documents cut to what staff can read

- Direction from the winery: the policies and procedures were too many and too long for staff to read and remember, so they are being cut to the bare minimum that meets the obligations, and a document that satisfies more than one authority is kept once. The plan to import another long procedure as written was dropped.
- Each procedure was rewritten as one short page, worked from the industry code's model procedures, the auditor's document list and the project's own requirements list: breakage at bottling (from about 680 words to about 230), recall (about 1,050 to about 290), organic handling (about 880 to about 310) and the health and safety policy (about 2,700 to about 830). One further page states, once, how long records are kept and where each lives.
- The record-keeping period was checked against the project's own notes and a web search: only one period is actually recorded, so the page says to keep records longer if an authority asks, and the rest is a question for the auditor.
- The import script's tests changed from "every document states the period" to "the policy and the records page state it, and no document states a different one".
- The short versions went into the library as drafts, each with a dry run first and a second dry run confirming nothing could be added twice. The older long drafts stay until a person deletes them, because the import script never deletes.
- Pasting the dev server's console into the chat exposed a login credential earlier in the same stretch of work (a dev-mode logging behaviour); noted here as a warning.
- Not done: the remaining procedures and an audit checklist wait for inputs; nothing released.
