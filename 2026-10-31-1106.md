# 31 Oct 2026 — The stock at the financial year end, for the accountant

_Filtered copy: quantities of wine are left out._

**Asked (Mike):** reviewing the accountant's draft 2026 accounts, the one thing missing was a stock summary at 30 June 2026. He shared the Stock tracking sheet and said: "We're now keeping this in the app where we can anticipate a requirement to provide a stock summary on 30th June each year." Then: "Yes, build it."

**Done outside the repository:** this year's 30 June figures for the accountant, worked out from the sheet and checked against what was reported for 30 June 2025 (the signed statements and the FY2025 stock workbook). They are company finances, so they are kept out of this repository and this log, as the sales figures were on 30 October.

**Found first:**
- **The stock sheet's financial years end on 30 June, not 31 March.** Its FY2025 and FY2026 sold figures match VITIFI's July-to-June sign-outs exactly, and not April-to-March. "Where it went" (30 Oct) grouped by years to 31 March, and its comment said that was the sheet's. Wrong; fixed here.
- **VITIFI already knew the bottles on any day.** `stock_expected(place, day)`, written for stock takes, gives the latest count to then plus everything after. For 30 June 2026 it agrees exactly with the sheet for every 2022-2024 wine, and within a handful of bottles for 2021 (the same few sample dates found on 30 Oct).
- **The bulk wine on a day needed adding up.** `vessel_lot_balance_on` gives one vessel; nothing gave all of them by vintage and owner.
- **The 2025 vintage does not add up on 30 June 2026.** The barrel-downs from tanks are recorded but the tank fills before them are not, so dozens of vessels read below nothing while the barrels read full. A net figure would show almost nothing in the cellar. The 2025 cellar records are known to be incomplete (`docs/chronology.md`, racking); now the screen says so for any vintage it happens to.

**Decided (no question needed):**
- The year end is a setting, `financial_year_end` in `app_settings`, chosen by an owner or winemaker the first time Stock, "Year end" is opened. No winery's year end in code.
- No new rule for what was there on a day: bottles by `stock_expected`, bulk by `vessel_lot_balance_on` added up in a new `bulk_wine_on`. A vessel left below nothing is flagged, never netted.
- Bottles and litres only. The value stays the accountant's; costing is still a gap.

**Built,** on branch `claude/kahiwi-wines-financial-review-6ed29f`:
- **Migration** `20261105110000_bulk_wine_on.sql`: `bulk_wine_on(date)` (as the caller, closed to the public key) and `verify_bulk_wine_on()` (rolled back). Waits for Mike's yes.
- **Stock, "Year end"** (`YearEnd.tsx`, owners and winemakers): choose the year end; the stock at the end of the latest five year ends; in bottle by vintage ("Each wine", with when each was last counted); your own wine in tanks and barrels, clients' apart; what left bottle in the year; "Download for the accountant".
- **The workbook** (`yearEndWorkbook.ts`, exceljs): the summary, then the stock sheet's layout, each wine with sampled, sold, exported and other in each financial year from its first, and the notes.
