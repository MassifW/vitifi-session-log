## Bottling tab: not started, on purpose

A bottling writes to the volume ledger: it empties barrels or a tank, and
when the sources are several lots (a destination blend's barrels) it needs
the blend's lot and its parentage. Pressing writes lot_parentage and never
corrects it; a bottling must be correctable, and the cellar role has no
delete on lot_parentage. How the wine physically reaches the line decides
whether the blend should be recorded first (a racking into a tank) or at
bottling. Four questions asked rather than guessed.
