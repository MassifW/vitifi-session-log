# Irrigation: pause buttons, leaks and breakages, one gap list per vineyard

29 October 2026 (UTC; 30 October morning in New Zealand). Cloud session, branch `mike/irrigation-pauses-and-leaks`.

## Asked

"The Clos Miki irrigation is scheduled. Sometimes we pause these, either as a group or individually. We need to insert pause
buttons into the app and the ability to note where leaks or breakages occur that may have affected the irrigation cycle. The
longest without water list should seperate Pisa and Clos Miki."

## Built

- `supabase/migrations/20261102100000_irrigation_pauses_and_faults.sql`: `irrigation_schedule_pauses` (schedule, paused from,
  resumes at or null until resumed, group_ref shared by a group pause, notes; no overlap between two pauses of one schedule;
  the schedule must be the row's own winery's) and `irrigation_faults` (vineyard, zone if known, leak / breakage / other,
  where in words, found on, fixed on, notes; zone on the same vineyard; not found in the future, NZ date). Both tenant tables
  born with winery_id, tenant_fill and the restrictive wall; RLS read for anyone signed in, write/edit/delete for the vineyard
  roles; anon revoked. Export views `export_irrigation_pauses` and `export_irrigation_faults`.
- A pause is a row, not a flag on the schedule, so ended pauses stay readable beside the runs.
- `app/lib/irrigationSchedule.ts`: `IrrigationPause`, `currentPause`, `comingPause`, `pauseText`, `checkPause`;
  `nextStart` / `lastStart` take the pauses and skip held-back starts (old callers unchanged); `IrrigationFault`,
  `FAULT_KINDS`, `checkFault`, `shortDay`; `gapRows` carries `paused` and `openFaults`; `gapRowsBySite`.
- `irrigationScheduleActions.ts`: `getIrrigationPlan` (schedules, pauses, faults in one ask), `pauseIrrigationSchedules`,
  `resumeIrrigationSchedules`, `deleteIrrigationPause`, `saveIrrigationFault`, `deleteIrrigationFault`.
- Screens: Pause / Resume per timer, Pause all / Resume all per vineyard, a pause sheet (from when; until Resume or a day;
  why); "Leaks and breakages" section (report, Fixed, change, delete behind the typed word); "Longest without water" with a
  heading per vineyard, "Timer paused", and unfixed leaks under their zone. IrrigationTab now loads the plan once and hands it
  down, where the schedule list and the gap list each used to ask for the schedules (two queued server actions became one).
- Export list, Drive copy, tenancy inventory (the test generalised from one hard-coded later table to a list, each checked
  for its column, fill and wall), manual, chronology, your-data, file-directory, open-items.

## Choices made without asking

- "As a group" = every timer at one vineyard; the schedules carry no timer-node name to group by.
- Vineyard hands may pause and record leaks, not only owners and winemakers.
- Manual (tap) areas cannot be paused; pausing has no offline hold.

## Later in the session: fertigation

Asked: "It's not something that we've historically tended to do but we should add the ability to record fertigation."

- Decided to make it a new event type `fertigation` in the vineyard book, not a new table and not a field on the spray
  record. The spray form is built around a sprayer (tank size, tanks, water rate) that fertigation does not have, and
  sprays are where the withholding-period checks live. The product comes from the existing register (which already has a
  `fertiliser_or_nutrient` category). The amount stored is the total put in. A rate per hectare is worked out from the
  zone's area, never stored.
- `20261102110000_fertigation.sql`: `event_domain` with `fertigation` as vineyard; `fertigation_valid()` trigger;
  `export_fertigation`; `export_other_records` and `verify_export_completeness` redefined (copied from their latest
  versions, one line each added).
- `app/lib/fertigation.ts`, `app/lib/fertigationActions.ts` (folded into `getIrrigationPlan`, so still one ask),
  `app/components/Irrigation/Fertigation.tsx`, "Fertigate" on a running zone, manual, docs, `tests/fertigation.test.mjs`.
- Verified: `npm test` 2,057 pass / 0 fail / 70 live skipped; typecheck and build clean (the build's one warning, in
  `scripts/backup/drive-tree.mjs`, is there without this change too). Trigger, domain and export view tried on a
  throwaway Postgres with stubs: 8 bad records and a bad edit refused in words, export rates right. The two redefined
  objects were not executed there.
- Open for Mike: whether fertigation should go into the spray-diary export the SWNZ audit reads ("application method …
  fertigation" is one of its fields), Trace, the Calendar and the Season tab.
- Public copy: the same as above, except product names stay out of it as inputs; none are named here, so it can be
  copied as is.
