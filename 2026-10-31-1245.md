# 31 Oct 2026 — A Safety layer on the Map

**Asked (for Mike, after the H&S maps were redrawn this morning):** he wants to move the safety features on the maps himself. He chose editable slides now and "the app later": a layer on the Map tab where an owner, a winemaker or the H&S lead places, drags, edits and deletes safety points, so their positions are each winery's own data, not a file. Seed today's points once the table is live. Design the printed map for Mike to agree, but don't build it.

**Decided (my call, written up in the decisions log):**
- **Their own table, not events.** An event is something done on a day and kept as it was. A safety point is a fixture that gets moved and reworded, and the people who keep the H&S documents look after it, not the roles that record field and cellar work.
- They aren't features of an uploaded map layer either. Those belong to a file someone brought in.
- Each point is attached to the vineyard it's at, so it belongs to whoever owns that vineyard. Nothing about any winery's layout goes in the code.
- **A point can hold several kinds.** Kits that share a shed are four things at one spot, and four points at exactly the same spot would hide each other on the map.
- Twelve kinds: assembly area, first aid kit, fire extinguisher, spill kit, toilet and hand-wash, H&S information, gate, water tank, and hazards (deep water, drop-off, vehicles, general).
- Each kind gets an ISO 7010-style sign: a green square for safe conditions, a red square for fire equipment, a yellow triangle for warnings, a blue circle for "read this". Its name always shows beside it, never colour alone.
- Dragging needs "Move" first, and deleting needs a typed word. Neither can happen from a stray tap.

**Built**, on a branch:
- **The migration:**
  - The table is walled per winery like every other winery record.
  - Everyone signed in can read it. Only owners, winemakers and the H&S lead can write it, and the public key gets nothing.
  - Who placed a point, and when, can't be written from the app.
  - It comes with an export view and a test harness that checks each rule and is rolled back afterwards.
- **On the Map:**
  - A "Safety" button sits beside the zoom buttons. Tap a point to see what it is; "Key" lists the signs on the map.
  - "Add a point" means tapping the map, ticking what's there and naming it. Points can also be edited, moved and deleted.
  - On a phone the card sits under the map, which is too short to hold a form on top. On a wide screen the card sits over the map.
  - With no signal, the phone shows the points as it last saw them and says when that was.
- **Records:** a new file in the "All records" download and the Drive copy. The data guide, the file directory, the chronology, the decisions log, the manual and the open items are all updated. There's a new test file.
- **The seed** is a one-off script that lives outside the repository with its data, so no winery's layout is in the code. It shows what it would do before it writes anything, writes everything in one go, and refuses to run twice. Which points went where is in the private log only, because it describes the property's layout.

**Verified**, on a local copy of last night's backup with the new migration on top:
- The harness passes 20 of 20, the tenancy check is clean, and the export view survives a restore.
- **In a browser:**
  - As an owner: added, moved, edited and deleted a point, and checked each in the database.
  - As a vineyard hand: saw the points with no edit buttons. Made the H&S lead, the same login got them.
  - When reading failed, the card said "could not be read", not "none placed".
  - At phone width.
- The seed script wrote the points on the copy and refused a second run.
- **The full test suite** against the copy: before bringing the branch up to date, one failure that was already on `main` (a contract test that misread one line of the Xero client as a table name). After merging the newer `main`, which fixed it, all 2,410 pass, with every live-database suite run.
