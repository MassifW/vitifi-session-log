# 30 Oct 2026 — Xero stock adjustments from VITIFI: a proposal

**Asked (Mike):** stock not invoiced in Xero is adjusted by hand there (samples, tap to pay, website and WCD sales), with the same wine, bottles, date and reason already signed out in VITIFI, so it is typed twice. Could VITIFI talk to Xero? Long term, replacing Xero would be ideal.

**Written:** `docs/xero.md` (proposal, nothing built), with pointers from `bottling-and-stock.md`, `chronology.md`, `open-items.md` (a question and suggestion 17) and `file-directory.md`.

**Found:**
- It reverses a principle: CLAUDE.md (6 Oct) says VITIFI does not write to Xero, and `decisions.md` held writing to Xero back "with the accountant". Not changed; waiting for Mike's yes.
- Xero's API has no stock adjustment call. Its own developer guide ("Integrating with Xero tracked inventory", read in the browser pane because the page is drawn by script) says: a zero-total supplier credit note (`ACCPAYCREDIT`) to a contact "Inventory Adjustments", two lines per item, the item at its average cost to the inventory account and the same negative to the adjustment account; an increase is the same as a zero-total bill (`ACCPAY`).
- The winery's Xero (read through Claude's Xero connector, chart of accounts only, nothing else read): 630 Inventory (type INVENTORY, so tracked items), 440 Inventory adjustments ("such as when bottles are taken for tasting"), 310 Cost of Goods Sold, and 632/633 Stock - Opening/Closing Balance, which suggests (not confirmed) a year-end stock journal by the accountant.
- Xero's developer pricing since 2 March 2026: Starter free for 5 connections, Core A$35/month for 50, Plus A$245/month for 1,000 (developer.xero.com/pricing, via search; not opened directly).

**Proposed:** connect per winery (OAuth, keys kept where no login can read them); read-only comparison of Xero's count with VITIFI's first; each sign-out reason carries whether it goes to Xero and to which account (rows, not code); a "Not yet in Xero" list and one send button, one credit note per sign-out, status shown per sign-out; corrections sent as the difference. The alternative for the accountant: turn off Xero's stock tracking and let VITIFI be the count. Long term: VITIFI can take the stock and invoices from Xero, not the ledger.

**Uncertain:** that Xero's UI "Adjust" button records differently from the API's credit note (third-party guides, not Xero's own page); whether a zero-total credit note can be voided (not needed by the design, which sends differences); the 60-day lapse of Xero's refresh token is from memory, not re-checked today.

**Gates:** docs only; `tests/reference-docs.test.mjs` and the suite run before commit.
