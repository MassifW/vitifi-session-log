# 2026-10-06 — Bottling, bottled stock, sign-outs and sales: proposal

Mike: "Let's look at bottling and stock management." Today a bottling is
recorded on the barrel log (a static copy of the Cat.Dropdown tab, details
in comments) with a bottling folder per vintage; bottled wine is stacked
in the bottled cellar (the "Bottled Cellar Map" sheet) and entered in a
stock sheet ("Stock tracking"). He wants an easy sign-out (sample or sale,
by whom, what for, when) instead of the paper record and hand
reconciliations, and sales tracked by where the wine went and what it
sold for — per category, channel and market. They sell DTC, to trade,
distributors, staff and overseas, and invoice in Xero. Mid-session: "our
website (squarespace) sales, tap to pay and samples need to be entered
into xero manually... there are always question marks at stocktake around
wines that have been removed but not yet accounted for etc".

## Read

- Both Google Sheets through the Drive connector (read only). The cellar
  map: a plan per month since May 2025, wines stacked by bay, dry goods on
  the same plan; a Stocklist tab with current counts and a "written out"
  log (name, date, number, reason). Stock tracking: per wine code (NM21,
  NM21MAG ...) litres bottled, stock after bottling, E-cert batch,
  sampled/sold/exported, calculated vs counted at 19 stock takes since
  mid-2024 with discrepancies (figures withheld from this copy), FY totals; a samples tab and a sold tab per
  vintage (date, bottles per code, reason or customer, signed, NZ/export
  and country, "Entered Customs" and "Entered Xero" ticks, NZ Wine Levy
  quarter); stock held at distributors; a blank monthly sign-out form.
- `data/source/2024/2024 Bottling and dry goods.xlsx`: eight bottling
  forms for the 2024 wines (Jan-Feb 2026): times, gases, filter, dry goods
  spec, screwcap QC checks, breakages, pre/post free SO2, CO2 and temp,
  bottles filled, lab, retention and winemaker bottles marked "excise
  exempt" per run, bottles in stock, QC and manager, the scheme
  membership numbers (withheld here: certification details), bottler's
  reference; all cleanskin. Counts are withheld from this copy. A stock list and
  a dry goods run sheet with glass and closure batches per run.
- In the app: the ledger's `bottling` movement type reserved and unused;
  analyses (built today) cover the bottling chemistry; documents; people.
  Nothing counted in bottles.
- Checked: Customs' "Excise entries" page (samples and tastings used in
  the CCA are included in an entry; monthly unless approved for 6 or 12);
  Xero's API pricing (Starter tier free, up to 5 connections); Squarespace
  Commerce API (orders API needs Commerce Advanced outside the US; order
  CSV export on any commerce plan). The Customs guide PDF for a
  manufacturing CCA returned 404 and was not read.
