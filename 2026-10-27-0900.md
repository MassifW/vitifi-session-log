# 27 Oct 2026: first-run tips, and a winemaking-only start

- **Asked:** make the app easy to start for a brand new user with no pre-loaded data: a welcome after installation and a short explanation the first time each tab is opened. Then: some users will want only the growing half of the app (ending at harvest), others mainly the winemaking half.
- **Built:** a welcome card and a per-tab card, dismissed once per person and kept on the device (nothing recorded about anyone); a "Hide all tips" and a "Tips" button to start again. The parts of the app are all optional now, with a winemaking-only start beside the grower's simple start, and the app opens on the first tab shown. The words are tested: every tab must have a card, and every button a card names must exist.
- **Found:** the app has no way to create a vineyard or block, and nothing separates one winery's records from another's, so a brand new user's own empty copy needs a first-run set-up and a decision on one copy per winery or one shared system. Tips alone do not solve it.
- **Open:** whether harvest belongs on both sides of the app.
- **Later:** harvest now sits on both sides of the app as the handover between growing and winemaking. The manual's "Your data" section was rewritten for a first-time user: where records are held, who can see them, how to have them without the app, and how the exported files are named. Ideas noted, not built: a skippable vineyard set-up, a one-click audit pack, a user-chosen cloud copy, and a comparison page against other winery software.
