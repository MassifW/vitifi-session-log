# 2026-10-04 20:40 — Growth walks: the stage pictures while choosing, and where each photo was taken

The owner's first walk with the app worked well. A photo taken with the
general Photo button was placed in the right row with no signal at all.
He asked for two things:
- the field guide's stage pictures shown while choosing a stage, not only
  after, so they guide rather than confirm;
- photos taken on a walk to record where they were taken, as that button's
  photos do. They didn't: they named only their parcel.

## The stage list

- A drop-down can't show a picture. So on a walk and on a correction,
  choosing an EL stage now opens a list over the screen: every stage in
  order, the guide's picture beside each one it has, and no empty frame
  for the rest.
- It opens at the stage chosen, or else at the one last recorded for that
  parcel.
- A magnifier shows a picture larger, with "Choose this stage" under it.
- "Skip this parcel", "as suggested" and "no EL number" are offered where
  they were before.
- The pictures are kept on the phone, so the list works with no signal.

## Where each walk photo was taken

- **On the phone.** The photo's own location is used where it has one,
  read before the photo is shrunk, since shrinking drops it. Otherwise
  the phone's position just after the photo comes back: a close fix at
  once, else the best in 20 seconds, else none. The phone is asked only
  then, never in the camera's own tap, using the same code the Photo
  button uses.
- **With the walk.** Each place is held with the walk, so a walk saved
  without signal keeps it. A photo lost on the phone takes its place with
  it.
- **In the database.** The function that saves a walk checks each place as
  it checks the photos, in the same words as the phone. It keeps the
  place, and works out the parcel and nearest row with the same function
  as the Photo button.
- **Whether the photo was in the reading's own parcel.** Added after the
  browser test: a photo taken in the next block would otherwise have shown
  that block's row number under this one. The list now shows:
  - the row, for a photo in the reading's own parcel;
  - the block as well, when the reading covers more;
  - the parcel it was actually taken in, when that's somewhere else.

## Found along the way

  data was right and the check was wrong; fixed.
- The hidden browser panel stopped bringing the page to life after a
  reload, as it did this morning. So the "taken somewhere else" caption
  was tested as a function rather than on screen.
